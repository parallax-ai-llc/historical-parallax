## Summary

Lil Nas X (born Montero Lamar Hill, April 9, 1999) is an American rapper, singer, and songwriter who rose from internet obscurity to global fame with "Old Town Road" in 2019. The country-rap single spent 19 weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot 100, the longest run in the chart's history at the time, and earned him two Grammy Awards.

He matters beyond one hit. Lil Nas X came out as gay at the peak of that song's success, then built a career on provocative, openly queer imagery in hip-hop and pop, most notably with "Montero (Call Me by Your Name)" and "Industry Baby." His command of memes, TikTok, and social media marketing has become a template for how artists launch music in the streaming era.

## Early Life & Background

Montero Hill was born in Lithia Springs, Georgia. His parents divorced when he was about six, and he later moved to Austell, Georgia, to live with his father, a gospel singer. He graduated from Lithia Springs High School in 2017 and briefly attended the University of West Georgia before dropping out to pursue music.

As a teenager he spent much of his time online, running popular fan accounts and posting viral comedy content on Twitter. That experience with internet culture later shaped his approach to promotion.

## Rise to Prominence

In December 2018, he independently released "Old Town Road," built on a beat purchased for about $30 that sampled Nine Inch Nails. The song spread through TikTok's "Yeehaw Challenge" and charted simultaneously on Billboard's Hot 100, Hot Country Songs, and Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs. In March 2019, Billboard removed it from the country chart, igniting a national debate about genre and race.

He signed with Columbia Records that same month. A remix featuring Billy Ray Cyrus, released in April 2019, propelled the song to number one, where it remained for 19 consecutive weeks. On June 30, 2019, the last day of Pride Month, he publicly came out as gay. His debut EP, *7*, followed and produced the top-five hit "Panini."

## Key Achievements

- "Old Town Road" earned two Grammy Awards in January 2020 (Best Pop Duo/Group Performance and Best Music Video) and became one of the highest-certified singles in RIAA history.
- He won Musical Event of the Year at the 2019 Country Music Association Awards, becoming the first openly gay Black artist to win a CMA award.
- "Montero (Call Me by Your Name)" (March 2021) and "Industry Baby" with Jack Harlow (July 2021) both reached number one on the Hot 100.
- His debut album *Montero* (September 2021) debuted at number two on the Billboard 200 and received five Grammy nominations, including Album of the Year.
- He won MTV Video Music Awards for Song of the Year (2019) and Video of the Year (2021).

## Controversies & Criticism

The "Montero" video, which depicted him giving Satan a lap dance, and the accompanying "Satan Shoes" made with MSCHF (666 pairs containing a drop of human blood) drew condemnation from conservative and religious groups. Nike sued MSCHF for trademark infringement and the case settled with a recall. In January 2024, the single "J Christ" and its imagery of him as Jesus prompted renewed backlash, and he posted an apology to those offended.

His 2019 admission that he had run a Nicki Minaj fan account before fame drew accusations of dishonesty. In August 2025, he was arrested in Los Angeles and charged with felony counts including battery on a police officer. He pleaded not guilty the following month.

## Legacy & Influence

Lil Nas X demonstrated that a viral single with no label backing could dominate the charts, and his marketing approach has been widely studied and imitated. He also broadened the space for openly queer Black artists in hip-hop, a genre long criticized for homophobia, and his visibility helped shift industry attitudes about what mainstream success can look like.
