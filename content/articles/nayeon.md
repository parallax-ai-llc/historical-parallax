## Summary

Im Na-yeon, known professionally as Nayeon, is a South Korean singer, dancer and occasional actress. She is the oldest member of the girl group TWICE, which debuted under JYP Entertainment on October 20, 2015, and became one of the best-selling K-pop acts of the late 2010s and 2020s.

In June 2022 she became the first TWICE member to release solo music. Her debut EP, *Im Nayeon*, entered the US Billboard 200 at number seven, making her the first K-pop soloist to reach the top ten of that chart. A second EP, *Na*, followed in 2024 and also reached the top ten.

Nayeon's importance rests on two things: her role as a founding, front-facing member of TWICE, and her demonstration that a member of a Korean girl group could carry a commercially viable solo career while the group remained active.

## Early Life & Background

Nayeon was born on September 22, 1995, in Seoul, South Korea. As a child she appeared in a clothing advertisement after her mother entered her in a contest, an early brush with entertainment that she later cited as sparking her interest in performing.

In 2010 she placed second in the seventh JYP Entertainment open audition and joined the company as a trainee in September of that year. She went on to study at Konkuk University's Department of Film Arts, balancing her studies with years of vocal and dance training.

## Rise to Prominence

Nayeon trained for roughly five years. In 2014 she was slated to debut in a six-member group tentatively called 6mix, but the project was scrapped before launch. In 2015 she was placed in the survival program *Sixteen*, where she was the first trainee confirmed for the final lineup.

TWICE debuted on October 20, 2015, with the EP *The Story Begins* and the single "Like Ooh-Ahh". The group's breakthrough came in 2016 with "Cheer Up", which won Song of the Year at the Mnet Asian Music Awards, followed by "TT". Nayeon was frequently positioned as a lead vocalist, a center in choreography and the group's most visible face on variety television.

Her solo debut arrived on June 24, 2022, with the EP *Im Nayeon* and the lead single "Pop!". The record featured collaborations with Felix of Stray Kids, Wonstein and Stephanie Poetri. Her second EP, *Na*, was released on June 14, 2024, with the lead single "ABCD".

## Key Achievements

- **First TWICE soloist:** *Im Nayeon* opened at number seven on the Billboard 200, a first for any K-pop solo artist, and sold several hundred thousand copies in its first week in South Korea.
- **Second top-ten entry:** *Na* (2024) returned her to the Billboard 200 top ten and outsold her debut in its opening week domestically.
- **TWICE milestones:** As a member she shares in two consecutive MAMA Song of the Year awards (2016, 2017), multiple million-selling albums, and the group's status as the first K-pop girl group to headline US stadiums during the 2023 *Ready to Be* tour.
- **Acting and hosting:** She appeared in the 2015 web drama *Dream Knight* and has hosted music programs and award shows, building a reputation as a reliable live presenter.

## Controversies & Criticism

Nayeon has been the target of persistent stalking. In December 2019 a German national boarded a flight from Japan to Seoul that TWICE was taking and attempted to approach her. He continued to post about her online and later returned to South Korea. JYP Entertainment sought legal protection, and in 2022 the company confirmed it had obtained restraining measures and pledged stronger action against sasaeng behavior. Critics questioned whether the agency acted quickly enough, while others pointed to the case as evidence of the limits of Korean anti-stalking law before its 2021 revision.

Her solo work has drawn some criticism for leaning on a bright, "aegyo"-heavy concept that some reviewers felt did not stretch her artistically. Fans and critics also debated whether her chart success reflected TWICE's fanbase more than her individual appeal, a critique commonly aimed at idol soloists.

## Legacy & Influence

Nayeon's solo debut set a benchmark for Korean girl group members launching solo careers in the US market. Her Billboard 200 entry in 2022 was cited when later soloists such as Jisoo, Rosé and Jennie of Blackpink reached the same chart. Within TWICE, she remains a defining presence, and her continued dual activity as group member and soloist has become a model that JYP applied to subsequent members.
