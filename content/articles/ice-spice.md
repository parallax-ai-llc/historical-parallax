## Summary

Ice Spice, born Isis Naija Gaston on January 1, 2000, is an American rapper from the Bronx, New York City. She emerged from the Bronx drill scene in 2022 and became one of the fastest-rising artists in hip-hop within a single year, driven largely by short-form video platforms and a distinctive, understated delivery over drill production.

Her breakout single "Munch (Feelin' U)" (2022) turned a slang term into a cultural shorthand, and a run of 2023 collaborations with PinkPantheress, Nicki Minaj, and Taylor Swift placed her in the top five of the Billboard Hot 100 repeatedly. She received four nominations at the 66th Grammy Awards, including Best New Artist.

Ice Spice's importance lies in how quickly internet virality translated into mainstream chart and industry success, and in her role as the most visible face of New York drill for a global audience.

## Early Life & Background

Isis Naija Gaston was born in the Bronx to a Dominican mother and a Nigerian-American father who had been an underground rapper. The eldest of five children, she grew up in the Fordham Road area of the Bronx after her parents divorced when she was two.

She attended Sacred Heart High School, a Catholic school in Yonkers, and later enrolled at the State University of New York at Purchase to study communications. At Purchase she played volleyball and met the producer RiotUSA, who would go on to produce nearly all of her early work. She left college before completing her degree to pursue music.

## Rise to Prominence

Ice Spice released her first track, "Bully Freestyle," in March 2021 after watching Bronx drill's rapid growth. A 2022 freestyle over Drake's "Munch" prompt on the "Buss It" challenge gained attention, and in August 2022 she released "Munch (Feelin' U)." The song went viral on TikTok, was played by Drake on his SiriusXM station, and entered the Billboard Hot 100 and the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop chart.

In early 2023 she signed with 10K Projects and Capitol Records and released her debut EP, *Like..?*, on January 20, 2023. It included "Bikini Bottom" and "In Ha Mood." Within months she featured on "Boy's a Liar Pt. 2" with PinkPantheress (peaking at number 3 on the Hot 100), the "Princess Diana" remix with Nicki Minaj (number 4), and the "Karma" remix with Taylor Swift (number 2).

## Key Achievements

- **"Barbie World" (2023)**, with Nicki Minaj and Aqua, from the *Barbie* soundtrack, reached number 7 on the Hot 100 and earned Grammy nominations for Best Rap Song and Best Song Written for Visual Media.
- **Four Grammy nominations** at the 66th Annual Grammy Awards in February 2024, including Best New Artist and Best Pop Duo/Group Performance for "Karma."
- **Best New Artist** at the 2023 MTV Video Music Awards.
- **Debut album *Y2K!*** released July 26, 2024, debuting at number 18 on the Billboard 200, led by the single "Think U the Shit (Fart)." It was supported by the Y2K! World Tour across North America and Europe.
- Named to the *Time* 100 Next list in 2023 and fronted a widely viewed Dunkin' campaign alongside Ben Affleck in 2023.

## Controversies & Criticism

Critics have questioned her technical range, describing her verses as minimal and her hooks as repetitive, a critique she has addressed by pointing to her deliberate laid-back style. The Taylor Swift "Karma" remix drew scrutiny in May 2023 after Swift's then-partner Matty Healy was recorded laughing at derogatory jokes about Ice Spice on a podcast; some observers framed the collaboration as damage control, which both artists denied.

In 2024 she faced online commentary about her weight loss and a public falling-out with former friend and collaborator Cleotrapa, who accused her of poor treatment while on tour. Her 2024 single "Did It First" with Central Cee also drew mixed reviews as *Y2K!* underperformed relative to expectations set by her singles run.

## Legacy & Influence

Ice Spice demonstrated how a drill artist could reach pop audiences without abandoning the genre's production, and she brought national attention to Bronx drill at a time when the scene faced heavy police and media scrutiny. Her nickname "the People's Princess," her signature curly red hair, and phrases such as "munch" became widely recognized cultural references. She has been cited as a template for the TikTok-to-chart pipeline that shaped hip-hop marketing in the mid-2020s.
