## Summary

Wizkid, born Ayodeji Ibrahim Balogun on 16 July 1990 in Lagos, is a Nigerian singer and songwriter widely regarded as one of the central figures in the global rise of Afrobeats. Rising from the Lagos church and street music scene, he became a national star with his 2011 debut album *Superstar* and a global one after his 2016 feature on Drake's "One Dance," which topped the Billboard Hot 100.

His 2020 album *Made in Lagos* and its single "Essence" (featuring Tems) marked a commercial turning point for African pop in the United States and Britain. "Essence" became the first Nigerian song to enter the Billboard Hot 100 and reached number nine after a Justin Bieber remix. He won his first Grammy in 2021 for "Brown Skin Girl" with Beyoncé.

Wizkid's career matters because it demonstrated that a Lagos-based artist could reach stadium scale in Europe and North America while singing in Yoruba, Nigerian Pidgin, and English, opening a path followed by a generation of West African musicians.

## Early Life & Background

Balogun was born in the Surulere district of Lagos to a Muslim father and a Christian mother and grew up in a large polygamous household. He began performing at around age 11 as part of a gospel group called the Glorious Five at his local church, which released an album while he was still a child.

As a teenager he recorded under the name Lil Prinz before adopting "Wizkid." He worked with Lagos producers and artists such as OJB Jezreel and Naeto C, learning studio craft while still in secondary school. He briefly attended Lagos State University and later Lead City University but left to pursue music full-time.

## Rise to Prominence

In 2009 Wizkid signed to Empire Mates Entertainment (EME), the label run by singer Banky W. His debut single "Holla at Your Boy" was released in 2010 and won Best Pop Single at the 2011 Headies. The album *Superstar* followed on 12 June 2011 and established him as one of Nigeria's biggest pop acts.

He left EME in 2013 and founded Starboy Entertainment. His second album, *Ayo* (17 September 2014), included "Ojuelegba," a song about his Surulere upbringing. A 2015 remix featuring Drake and Skepta brought him international attention, and in April 2016 Drake's "One Dance," featuring Wizkid and Kyla, spent ten weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot 100, making him the first Nigerian artist to appear on a US number-one single.

He signed with RCA Records in March 2017 and released *Sounds from the Other Side* that July. In 2018 he became the first African artist to sell out London's O2 Arena.

## Key Achievements

- **Albums:** *Superstar* (2011), *Ayo* (2014), *Sounds from the Other Side* (2017), *Made in Lagos* (2020), *More Love, Less Ego* (2022), and *Morayo* (2024), named after his late mother.
- **"Essence" (2020):** first Nigerian song to chart on the Billboard Hot 100, peaking at number nine in 2021; certified platinum by the RIAA; nominated for Best Global Music Performance at the 2022 Grammy Awards.
- **Grammy Award:** Best Music Video in 2021 for "Brown Skin Girl" with Beyoncé, Blue Ivy Carter, and Saint Jhn.
- **Live milestones:** three sold-out nights at the O2 Arena in late 2021 and a headline show at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on 29 July 2023 before more than 60,000 people.
- **Awards:** multiple BET Awards for Best International Act (2012, 2017), numerous MOBO Awards for Best African Act, an MTV Europe Music Award, and more than a dozen Headies.

## Controversies & Criticism

Wizkid's split from EME in 2013 was followed by public friction with Banky W and label mates. His long-running rivalry with fellow Nigerian star Davido has repeatedly spilled onto social media, including heated exchanges in 2017 and again in 2024.

In 2018 the mothers of two of his children publicly accused him of neglecting parental responsibilities, which he disputed. He has also faced criticism for late arrivals and no-shows at concerts, including a 2021 performance in Ghana that ended abruptly and a 2023 postponement of his North American tour that left fans seeking refunds. In 2023 and 2024 he drew backlash from Nigerian fans and peers for remarks that distanced himself from the "Afrobeats" label and described the genre as limiting.

## Legacy & Influence

Wizkid is credited, alongside Burna Boy and Davido, with turning Afrobeats from a regional sound into a global commercial category. His collaborations with Drake, Beyoncé, Skepta, and Tems created templates for cross-Atlantic partnerships, and *Made in Lagos* is frequently cited as the album that proved an African pop record could sustain long chart runs in the US and UK. His success helped push major labels to open African divisions and contributed to the creation of a dedicated Billboard Afrobeats chart in 2022. Younger artists such as Tems, Rema, and Asake have named him a direct influence.
