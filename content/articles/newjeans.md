## Summary

NewJeans is a South Korean girl group formed by ADOR, a subsidiary of HYBE, that debuted on 22 July 2022 with the single "Attention". The five members are Minji, Hanni, Danielle, Haerin and Hyein, who ranged in age from 14 to 18 at debut. The group was developed under creative director Min Hee-jin, and its name is a pun on "new genes" and on jeans as a timeless wardrobe staple.

The group became one of the fastest-rising acts in K-pop history. Its debut EP set a first-week sales record for a debut girl group, and its second EP, *Get Up* (2023), topped the Billboard 200. NewJeans stood out for a Y2K-influenced visual identity, understated choreography and a sound drawing on UK garage, Jersey club and 1990s R&B rather than the maximalist production common in the genre.

From 2024 the group was at the center of a public conflict between ADOR's leadership and HYBE that led the members to declare their contracts terminated, lose a court ruling and eventually return to the label. The dispute became one of the most closely watched labor and governance cases in the Korean entertainment industry.

## Early Life & Background

The members were recruited through HYBE's trainee system. Minji (Kim Min-ji, born 2004) and Haerin (Kang Hae-rin, born 2006) are from South Korea. Hanni (Pham Ngoc Han, born 2004) is Vietnamese-Australian, raised in Melbourne. Danielle (Danielle Marsh, born 2005) is Korean-Australian from Newcastle, New South Wales. Hyein (Lee Hye-in, born 2008) previously performed in the child groups U.S.SO Girl and Play With Me Club.

Min Hee-jin, formerly art director at SM Entertainment, joined HYBE in 2019 and became CEO of ADOR in 2021. She oversaw the group's concept, styling and marketing, launching NewJeans without teasers and releasing four music videos in the group's first week.

## Rise to Prominence

The debut EP *New Jeans* (1 August 2022) sold about 310,000 copies in its first week. "Attention" and "Hype Boy" both reached number one on the Circle Digital Chart. The single "Ditto" (December 2022) became the group's breakout hit, spending 14 weeks at number one on the Circle Digital Chart and giving NewJeans its first entry on the Billboard Hot 100. The single album *OMG* followed in January 2023.

*Get Up* (21 July 2023) sold more than 1.6 million copies in its first week and debuted at number one on the Billboard 200, making NewJeans the second K-pop girl group to top the chart. The group performed at Lollapalooza Chicago in August 2023 and recorded "GODS", the League of Legends World Championship anthem, with Riot Games. In June 2024 NewJeans released its Japanese debut single "Supernatural" and held two fan-meeting shows at Tokyo Dome, the fastest any K-pop act had reached the venue after debut.

## Key Achievements

- Highest first-week sales for a K-pop girl group debut album at the time (2022).
- Billboard 200 number one with *Get Up* (2023).
- Ranked eighth on the IFPI Global Artist Chart for 2023.
- Song of the Year at the 2023 MAMA Awards and Artist of the Year at the 2023 Melon Music Awards for "Ditto".
- Named to Time's 100 Next list in 2023.
- Members hold ambassador roles with Chanel, Gucci, Burberry, Dior and Louis Vuitton.

## Controversies & Criticism

In August 2022 the lyrics of "Cookie" drew accusations of sexual innuendo involving minors; ADOR denied any such intent. In April 2024 HYBE audited Min Hee-jin, alleging she planned to take ADOR independent, and Min responded with televised press conferences. A court blocked HYBE from dismissing her in May 2024, but the ADOR board replaced her as CEO on 27 August 2024. The members publicly demanded her reinstatement in September, and Hanni testified before the National Assembly in October 2024 about alleged workplace harassment.

On 28 November 2024 the members announced they were terminating their contracts, and in 2025 they briefly performed as NJZ. The Seoul Central District Court granted ADOR an injunction in March 2025 and ruled in October 2025 that the contracts remained valid. In December 2025 all five members announced they would return to ADOR.

## Legacy & Influence

NewJeans reshaped the sound and look of fourth-generation K-pop, popularizing "easy listening" production and nostalgic Y2K aesthetics adopted by many later groups. The contract dispute prompted broader debate about idol labor rights and the power balance between artists, sub-labels and conglomerates.
