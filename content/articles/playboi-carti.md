## Summary

Playboi Carti, born Jordan Terrell Carter on September 13, 1996, is an American rapper, singer, and model from the Atlanta area. He emerged from the mid-2010s SoundCloud scene with a minimalist, ad-lib-heavy delivery and became one of the most commercially successful and stylistically imitated artists of his generation.

His 2020 album *Whole Lotta Red* debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and, after an initially divided reception, came to be regarded as a defining text of the "rage" subgenre. His 2025 album *Music* debuted at number one with roughly 298,000 first-week units, the largest hip-hop opening of that year at the time of release.

Beyond his records, Carti founded the label Opium, which launched Ken Carson and Destroy Lonely, and he has worked as a runway and campaign model for luxury fashion houses.

## Early Life & Background

Carter was born in Atlanta, Georgia, and raised in Riverdale, a suburb south of the city. He attended North Springs Charter High School in Sandy Springs, where he played basketball before losing interest in school and leaving to focus on music. He worked briefly at an H&M store during this period.

He began releasing music around 2011 under the name Sir Cartier, adopting the name Playboi Carti in 2013. Early tracks circulated on SoundCloud, and he became loosely affiliated with the Atlanta collective Awful Records, run by the producer Father.

## Rise to Prominence

In 2016 Carti signed with A$AP Rocky's creative agency AWGE and Interscope Records. His self-titled debut mixtape arrived on April 14, 2017, reaching number 12 on the Billboard 200. Its single "Magnolia," produced by Pi'erre Bourne, peaked at number 29 on the Hot 100, and "wokeuptolikethis*" featuring Lil Uzi Vert also charted.

*Die Lit*, released May 11, 2018, debuted at number three on the Billboard 200 and cemented his partnership with Bourne. He then spent more than two years teasing *Whole Lotta Red*, during which extensive leaks fueled fan frustration. The album, executive produced by Kanye West, was released on December 25, 2020, and opened at number one with about 100,000 units.

*Music*, released March 14, 2025, ran 30 tracks and featured Kendrick Lamar, The Weeknd, Future, Travis Scott, and Skepta, among others. As a featured artist, Carti earned his first Hot 100 number one with "Carnival" alongside Kanye West and Ty Dolla $ign in 2024. He also appeared on West's *Donda* (2021), Travis Scott's *Utopia* (2023), and The Weeknd's "Timeless" (2024).

He founded Opium in 2019, signing Ken Carson, Destroy Lonely, and Homixide Gang. In fashion, he walked Virgil Abloh's debut Louis Vuitton show in June 2018 and later appeared in campaigns for Givenchy and Louis Vuitton.

## Controversies & Criticism

Carti has faced several legal issues. In 2017 he was arrested at Los Angeles International Airport on a domestic battery allegation; charges were not pursued. In April 2020 he was arrested in Clayton County, Georgia, on drug and firearm charges after a traffic stop. In December 2022 he was arrested on a felony aggravated assault charge involving his then-pregnant girlfriend; he was released on bond.

His relationship with rapper Iggy Azalea, with whom he has a son born in 2020, drew public scrutiny after she accused him of neglecting their child. Critics and fans have also repeatedly faulted the years-long delays of *Whole Lotta Red* and *Music*, the postponement of the Antagonist Tour, and the abrupt stylistic shifts, including his high-pitched "baby voice," which initially polarized listeners.

## Legacy & Influence

Carti's sparse, repetitive hooks and treatment of the voice as texture rather than narrative reshaped expectations for what a rap vocal could be. *Whole Lotta Red* is widely credited with popularizing the distorted, punk-inflected "rage" sound adopted by a generation of younger artists, many of them on his own label. His deliberate scarcity, secrecy, and vampire-inspired visual identity have made him as influential in aesthetics and fandom culture as in music itself.
