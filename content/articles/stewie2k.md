## Summary

Jacky "Stewie2K" Yip (born January 7, 1998) is an American professional Counter-Strike player, best known as a rifler and in-game leader for Cloud9 and Team Liquid. He was a core member of the Cloud9 roster that won the ELEAGUE Major: Boston in January 2018, the first and, to date, only Major title won by a North American organization.

After Boston, Stewie2K joined Team Liquid, where he helped the team win the Intel Grand Slam Season 2 in 2019 in a record 63 days. During that stretch Liquid held the number one spot in the HLTV world rankings and won four consecutive top-tier events, establishing him as one of the most decorated North American players in the game's history.

Stewie2K matters beyond his trophies. He entered the scene as a divisive online pickup and became the emotional center of two championship rosters. His aggressive, high-variance style and vocal presence made him one of the most recognizable personalities in North American Counter-Strike.

## Early Life & Background

Yip was born in San Francisco, California, to a Chinese-American family. He grew up playing Counter-Strike casually and built his reputation almost entirely through online pick-up matches on the ESEA platform, rather than through an established amateur team. His in-game name references the Family Guy character Stewie Griffin, with the "2K" suffix added when the original name was taken.

By 2015 he had a reputation as a talented but volatile online player, known for trash talk and unorthodox aggression. He had almost no LAN experience when Cloud9 signed him in January 2016 at age 18, a move that drew skepticism from fans and analysts.

## Rise to Prominence

Stewie2K's first year on Cloud9 was uneven, but the team's 2017 additions of Tarik "tarik" Celik and Will "RUSH" Wierzba stabilized the roster. Cloud9 reached the final of ESL One Cologne 2017 and finished second at DreamHack Denver 2017.

The turning point came at the ELEAGUE Major: Boston in January 2018. Cloud9 defeated FaZe Clan 2–1 in the grand final, coming back from a 1–0 map deficit and winning the deciding map, Inferno, in double overtime. Stewie2K's clutch on the final map became one of the era's defining moments.

In March 2018 he and tarik left Cloud9 for the Brazilian core of SK Gaming, which became MIBR that June. The experiment produced few results, and in December 2018 he moved to Team Liquid, replacing Epitácio "TACO" de Melo.

## Key Achievements

- **ELEAGUE Major: Boston 2018** champion with Cloud9, the first North American Major win.
- **Intel Grand Slam Season 2 (2019)** with Team Liquid, secured in 63 days across IEM Sydney, DreamHack Masters Dallas, ESL Pro League Season 9, and ESL One Cologne.
- **ESL One Cologne 2019** champion, defeating Vitality in the final.
- **IEM Chicago 2019** and **BLAST Pro Series Los Angeles 2019** titles.
- Named to HLTV's Top 20 players of 2018.
- Served as in-game leader for Liquid in 2020 and 2021, and for Evil Geniuses in 2022.

## Controversies & Criticism

Stewie2K's 2016 signing was widely criticized because of his reputation for online toxicity, and his early results on Cloud9 fueled that debate. His departure from Cloud9 within weeks of the Major win drew accusations of disloyalty, as did his brief stint with MIBR.

His time as Liquid's in-game leader in 2020 and 2021 coincided with the team's decline from the top ranking, and analysts questioned whether calling suited his instinctive style. His 2022 tenure with Evil Geniuses ended with him benched in October 2022 after poor results, and his public deliberation over switching to Valorant frustrated some fans.

## Legacy & Influence

Stewie2K is the most successful North American Counter-Strike player of his generation by titles won. The 2018 Boston victory remains a milestone for the region, and Liquid's 2019 run set the fastest Grand Slam pace in the award's history. His path from anonymous online player to Major champion made him a model for pickup players, and his personality helped grow Counter-Strike's North American streaming audience.
