## Summary

Martin Garrix, born Martijn Gerard Garritsen on 14 May 1996, is a Dutch DJ and music producer who became one of the defining figures of the 2010s electronic dance music (EDM) boom. His 2013 single "Animals," released when he was 17, topped charts across Europe and turned him into an international headliner almost overnight.

He has been voted the world's number one DJ in the DJ Mag Top 100 DJs poll five times, first in 2016 at the age of 20, making him the youngest artist ever to hold that position. Beyond his own releases, he founded the label STMPD RCRDS in 2016 and has collaborated with pop artists including Dua Lipa, Bebe Rexha, Khalid, Usher and Troye Sivan.

Garrix's career illustrates both the reach of festival-driven EDM and the business questions it raised, most visibly in his legal dispute with his first label over ownership of his early recordings.

## Early Life & Background

Garritsen was born in Amstelveen, a suburb of Amsterdam. He began playing guitar at age eight and has cited watching Tiësto perform at the opening ceremony of the 2004 Athens Olympics as the moment he decided to become a DJ. He taught himself production software as a teenager and enrolled at the Herman Brood Academie, a Dutch vocational school for music production, graduating in 2014.

## Rise to Prominence

He released early tracks under the name Martin Garrix from 2012, including "BFAM" with Julian Jordan, and produced an official remix of Christina Aguilera's "Your Body" that year. He signed to the Dutch label Spinnin' Records as a minor.

"Animals," released in June 2013, reached number one on the UK Singles Chart and in Belgium and entered the top ten in more than ten countries. Garrix became the youngest artist to top the Beatport chart. Follow-ups such as "Wizard" with Jay Hardway and "Tremor" with Dimitri Vegas & Like Mike cemented his place on major festival main stages, including Ultra Music Festival and Tomorrowland.

In August 2015 he announced his departure from Spinnin' Records and its affiliated management company. In 2016 he signed a global deal with Sony Music International and launched STMPD RCRDS.

## Key Achievements

- Number one in the DJ Mag Top 100 DJs poll in 2016, 2017, 2018, 2022 and 2023.
- Chart hits including "In the Name of Love" with Bebe Rexha (2016), "Scared to Be Lonely" with Dua Lipa (2017), "There for You" with Troye Sivan (2017) and "Ocean" with Khalid (2018).
- "We Are the People," recorded with Bono and The Edge of U2, served as the official song of UEFA Euro 2020, played in 2021.
- Multiple MTV Europe Music Awards for Best Electronic act.
- The side project AREA21 with Maejor, which released the album "European Tour" in 2021.
- Long-running residencies in Ibiza and Las Vegas, and closing sets at Tomorrowland.

## Controversies & Criticism

Garrix's most significant dispute was with Spinnin' Records and MusicAllStars Management. He filed suit in 2015, arguing that he had been misled about his contracts while still a minor and that he should control his master recordings. A Dutch court ruled largely in his favour in 2017, and the parties later reached a settlement that returned the rights to his early catalogue, including "Animals."

Critics of the big-room EDM style have singled out "Animals" as an example of formulaic, festival-oriented production. The DJ Mag poll that has ranked him first has also been criticised within the industry as a popularity contest driven by fan campaigns rather than musical merit.

## Legacy & Influence

Garrix demonstrated that a teenage producer working from a bedroom setup could reach the top of the global dance industry, and his path influenced a generation of young Dutch producers. STMPD RCRDS has become a platform for emerging artists, and his contract fight is frequently cited in discussions of artist rights in electronic music. His crossover collaborations helped bring EDM structures into mainstream pop during the second half of the 2010s.
