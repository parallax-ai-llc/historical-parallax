## Summary

Alexander Wang is an American fashion designer and entrepreneur who founded his namesake label in New York in 2005 and served as creative director of the Paris house Balenciaga from 2012 to 2015. He built his reputation on a downtown aesthetic that mixed athletic and streetwear references with luxury tailoring, and his early success made him one of the most visible young designers of his generation.

Wang's importance lies in how quickly he turned a small knitwear line into a global brand and in how he helped normalize the "model off-duty" look of oversized T-shirts, leather, and sneakers as high fashion. His collaborations with H&M, Adidas, and Uniqlo extended his reach far beyond the runway.

His career has also been shaped by controversy. Allegations of sexual misconduct made public in late 2020 led to a period of withdrawal from the fashion calendar, and his return in 2022 was met with mixed reactions.

## Early Life & Background

Wang was born on December 26, 1983, in San Francisco, California, to parents of Taiwanese descent. His family ran a plastics manufacturing business, and he was the youngest of three children. He began sewing and designing as a teenager, staging his first fashion show at age 15 for his brother's wedding.

In 2002 he moved to New York to study at Parsons School of Design. While at Parsons he interned at Marc Jacobs, Teen Vogue, and Vogue. He left the program during his second year to launch his own business.

## Rise to Prominence

Wang started his label in 2005 with a small collection of cashmere knitwear made with the support of his family. A full women's ready-to-wear line followed in 2007, and the brand's slouchy tanks, shrunken leather jackets, and heeled boots quickly found a following among models and editors.

In 2008 he won the CFDA/Vogue Fashion Fund, which came with $200,000 in prize money and mentorship. He won the Swarovski Award for Womenswear Designer of the Year from the Council of Fashion Designers of America in 2009 and the Accessory Designer of the Year award in 2010. He launched the more affordable T by Alexander Wang line in 2009.

In November 2012 Kering appointed him creative director of Balenciaga, succeeding Nicolas Ghesquière. He presented his first collection for the house in February 2013 and his last in October 2015 after the company announced his departure that July.

## Key Achievements

- **Global brand growth:** Expanded from a knitwear capsule into a company with womenswear, menswear, accessories, and standalone stores in New York, Beijing, Tokyo, and other cities.
- **Mass collaborations:** His H&M collection in November 2014 sold out within hours in many stores. He designed the Adidas Originals by Alexander Wang line from 2016 to 2019 and a Uniqlo Heattech collection in 2018.
- **Corporate leadership:** In 2016 he became chairman and chief executive of his company, taking over roles previously held by family members.
- **Anti-counterfeiting:** In 2013 the company won a judgment of roughly $90 million against counterfeiters selling fake goods online.
- **Schedule innovation:** In 2018 the brand left the traditional New York Fashion Week calendar and began showing twice yearly in June and December.

## Controversies & Criticism

In 2015 a group of workers filed a lawsuit alleging poor conditions at a Manhattan production facility linked to the company. The case was later dismissed.

In December 2020 several men and transgender women publicly accused Wang of sexual assault and misconduct at nightclubs and parties, with some allegations dating to 2017. Wang initially called the claims false. In March 2021, after meeting with accusers represented by attorney Lisa Bloom, he issued a statement acknowledging that his behavior had been harmful and pledging to "set a better example." No criminal charges were filed.

His April 2022 runway return in Los Angeles's Chinatown drew criticism from some in the industry who felt the comeback came too soon.

## Legacy & Influence

Wang helped define the luxury streetwear vocabulary that dominated fashion in the 2010s. His blend of sportswear silhouettes, black leather, and a nightlife sensibility influenced both large houses and younger designers. As an Asian American founder who reached the top of a European luxury house before age 30, he also broadened the picture of who could lead a global fashion brand.
