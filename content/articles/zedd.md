## Summary

Zedd, born Anton Zaslavski on 2 September 1989, is a Russian-born German DJ, record producer and songwriter. He emerged from the early-2010s electro house scene and became one of the producers most responsible for merging festival-style electronic dance music with mainstream pop radio.

His 2012 single "Clarity" earned the Grammy Award for Best Dance Recording in 2014, and later collaborations such as "Break Free" with Ariana Grande, "Stay" with Alessia Cara and "The Middle" with Maren Morris and Grey all reached the top ten of the Billboard Hot 100. Beyond his own releases he has produced and remixed for Lady Gaga, Justin Bieber, Skrillex and others.

Zedd matters because his classically trained, melody-first approach gave EDM a songwriting discipline that carried the genre well past its 2012–2014 commercial peak. He remains a headline act at festivals and Las Vegas residencies more than a decade later.

## Early Life & Background

Zaslavski was born in Saratov in the Russian SFSR and moved with his family to Kaiserslautern, West Germany, when he was about three years old. Both parents were musicians: his father a guitarist and teacher, his mother a piano teacher. He began classical piano lessons at age four and later learned drums.

At 12 he joined the German metalcore band Dioramic as a drummer, recording and touring with the group through the mid-2000s. He has cited hearing the French duo Justice's 2007 album *Cross* as the moment he decided to move into electronic production, which he began in earnest around 2009.

## Rise to Prominence

Zedd first drew attention by winning two Beatport remix competitions, including one for Fatboy Slim's "Weapon of Choice," and by releasing early singles on Steve Aoki's Dim Mak label. Official remixes for Skrillex ("Scary Monsters and Nice Sprites") and Lady Gaga ("Born This Way," "Marry the Night") followed in 2011.

He signed to Interscope Records in 2012. His single "Spectrum," featuring Matthew Koma, topped the Billboard Dance Club Songs chart that year, and his debut album *Clarity* was released on 2 October 2012. The title track, featuring British singer Foxes, peaked at number eight on the Hot 100 in 2013. That same year he co-produced Justin Bieber's "Beauty and a Beat" and contributed production to Lady Gaga's *Artpop*.

## Key Achievements

- **Grammy Award**: "Clarity" won Best Dance Recording at the 56th Grammy Awards in January 2014.
- **Chart singles**: "Break Free" (2014) reached number four on the Hot 100; "Stay" (2017) peaked at number seven; "The Middle" (2018) peaked at number five and earned three Grammy nominations, including Record of the Year.
- **Albums**: *True Colors* debuted at number four on the Billboard 200 in May 2015. His third album, *Telos*, released on 30 August 2024 after a nine-year gap, featured John Mayer, Bea Miller, Remi Wolf and Muse.
- **Live**: He has held long-running Las Vegas residencies, headlined main stages at Coachella and Ultra, and launched his own Zedd in the Park festival in Los Angeles in 2019.

## Controversies & Criticism

In October 2019 Zedd said he had been "permanently banned" from China after liking a tweet from the animated series *South Park*, which had just aired an episode critical of Chinese censorship. The incident drew wide media coverage and led to cancelled Chinese bookings.

He has also been criticised by some dance-music fans for drifting toward radio pop after *Clarity*, a shift he has openly defended. A 2015 public feud with fellow producer Diplo, conducted largely over Twitter, briefly made headlines before both parties moved on.

## Legacy & Influence

Zedd helped define the "pop-EDM" template of the 2010s, in which a producer is credited as lead artist while guest vocalists carry the song. His use of classical harmony, layered piano and dynamic builds influenced a generation of producers including the Chainsmokers, Gryffin and Illenium. The multi-platinum certifications of "Stay" and "The Middle" show that his crossover formula endured after the initial EDM boom faded.
