## Summary

Micah Aaron Parsons (born May 26, 1999) is an American football linebacker and edge rusher for the Green Bay Packers of the National Football League (NFL). He played college football at Penn State, where he was a consensus All-American in 2019, and was selected 12th overall by the Dallas Cowboys in the 2021 NFL Draft. He was named NFL Defensive Rookie of the Year in 2021 and earned Pro Bowl selections in each of his first four seasons.

Parsons is widely regarded as one of the most disruptive defenders of his era because of his rare combination of speed, strength and positional versatility. He recorded at least 12 sacks in each of his first four NFL seasons, a feat no other player had achieved to begin a career, and reached 50 career sacks faster than almost any player in league history.

In August 2025, after a public contract dispute with Dallas, Parsons was traded to Green Bay and signed a four-year, $188 million contract that briefly made him the highest-paid non-quarterback in NFL history.

## Early Life & Background

Parsons was born in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania. He began his high school career at Central Dauphin High School before transferring to Harrisburg High School, where he played defensive end and running back and was named the 2017 Pennsylvania Player of the Year. A five-star recruit, he was ranked among the top defensive prospects in the country. He initially committed to Penn State in 2016, decommitted, and then re-committed in December 2017.

## Rise to Prominence

At Penn State, Parsons played linebacker under coach James Franklin. As a freshman in 2018 he led the team in tackles with 83 despite starting only one game. In 2019 he recorded 109 tackles, 14 tackles for loss, 5 sacks and 4 forced fumbles, earning consensus All-American honors and the Butkus-Fitzgerald Award as Big Ten Linebacker of the Year. He opted out of the 2020 season during the COVID-19 pandemic and declared for the NFL Draft.

Dallas selected him 12th overall in 2021. Used both at off-ball linebacker and as a pass rusher, he produced 13 sacks and 84 tackles as a rookie, was named first-team All-Pro and won Defensive Rookie of the Year, finishing second in Defensive Player of the Year voting.

## Key Achievements

- **Four straight 12-plus-sack seasons:** 13 sacks in 2021, 13.5 in 2022, 14 in 2023 and 12 in 2024, the first player to open a career with four such seasons.
- **All-Pro honors:** first-team All-Pro in 2021 and 2022, second-team in 2023.
- **Pro Bowl selections:** every season from 2021 through 2024.
- **Fastest to 50 sacks:** reached 50 career sacks in his 61st game in 2024, among the quickest in NFL history.
- **Record contract:** signed a four-year, $188 million deal with Green Bay on August 28, 2025, averaging $47 million per season.

## Controversies & Criticism

In 2020, a lawsuit filed by former Penn State player Isaiah Humphries alleged that Parsons and other teammates took part in hazing; Parsons was named in the complaint but was not a defendant, and the case against the named defendant was dismissed. Reports also surfaced of a 2016 incident at Harrisburg High School in which Parsons was accused of involvement in sending an explicit image; no charges were filed.

As a professional, Parsons has drawn criticism for his outspokenness on social media and on his podcast, including comments about opposing players and his own team's decisions. His 2025 standoff with Cowboys owner Jerry Jones, who claimed a handshake deal had been reached without Parsons's agent, became a months-long public feud that ended with a trade request and his departure from Dallas. Critics have also pointed to his limited postseason production in three Cowboys playoff losses.

## Legacy & Influence

Parsons helped redefine the modern hybrid defender, moving between linebacker and edge rusher in ways that forced offenses to account for him on every snap. His trade to Green Bay, which cost the Packers two first-round picks and defensive tackle Kenny Clark, and his record contract reset the market for elite pass rushers. In his first Green Bay season he continued his double-digit sack pace before a torn anterior cruciate ligament in November 2025 ended his year, leaving his long-term trajectory as one of the league's most closely watched storylines.
