## Summary

Taeyang, born Dong Young-bae on 18 May 1988, is a South Korean singer, songwriter, and dancer best known as the main vocalist of Big Bang, one of the most commercially successful and influential groups in K-pop history. Since his solo debut in 2008 he has also built one of the most respected solo careers among idol group members, establishing himself as a leading R&B voice in Korean popular music.

His significance lies in demonstrating that a group member could sustain a distinct artistic identity. Albums such as *Solar* (2010) and *Rise* (2014) set chart benchmarks for Korean solo artists in the United States, and "Eyes, Nose, Lips" became one of the defining Korean ballads of the 2010s. After more than two decades at YG Entertainment, he moved to The Black Label in 2022 and continued releasing music while remaining a member of Big Bang.

## Early Life & Background

Dong Young-bae was born in Uijeongbu, Gyeonggi Province, South Korea. He entered the entertainment industry as a child, appearing in the music video for Jinusean's "A-Yo" in 2000 and joining YG Entertainment as a trainee at the age of 12. He trained for roughly six years alongside G-Dragon, initially as part of a planned hip-hop duo. During this period he attended school while learning vocals, rap, and dance, and he has described his Christian faith as a constant influence on his outlook and music.

## Rise to Prominence

Taeyang debuted as a member of Big Bang on 19 August 2006. The group's breakthrough came in 2007 with "Lies," followed by hits including "Haru Haru" (2008), "Fantastic Baby" (2012), and "Bang Bang Bang" (2015). Within the group he served as main vocalist and lead dancer.

His solo career began on 22 May 2008 with the EP *Hot*, whose lead single "Only Look at Me" topped Korean charts. The EP won Best R&B and Soul Album at the 2009 Korean Music Awards. His first full-length album, *Solar*, was released on 1 July 2010 and reached number two on the US iTunes R&B chart, an early sign of international interest in Korean solo music.

## Key Achievements

- *Rise*, released 3 June 2014, debuted at number 112 on the Billboard 200 and topped the Billboard World Albums chart, then the highest Billboard 200 entry for a Korean solo artist.
- "Eyes, Nose, Lips" won Song of the Year at the 2014 Mnet Asian Music Awards and was covered by numerous artists across Asia and the West.
- His third album, *White Night* (16 August 2017), was supported by a world tour spanning Asia and North America.
- After completing mandatory military service from March 2018 to November 2019, he signed with The Black Label in December 2022. His January 2023 single "Vibe," featuring Jimin of BTS, entered the Billboard Hot 100, and the EP *Down to Earth* followed on 25 April 2023.
- Big Bang's cumulative record includes multiple Mnet Asian Music Awards for Artist of the Year and a MTV Europe Music Award for Best Worldwide Act in 2011.

## Controversies & Criticism

Taeyang has maintained a comparatively scandal-free personal record, but his career has been shaped by controversy around him. The lyrics of "Only Look at Me," in which the narrator asks a partner to stay faithful while admitting his own infidelity, drew criticism for a double standard, although the song's popularity was largely unaffected. Big Bang faced repeated scrutiny over member conduct, including G-Dragon's 2011 marijuana case, T.O.P's 2017 drug conviction, and Seungri's 2019 Burning Sun scandal, which led to Seungri's departure. Critics questioned whether the remaining members and YG Entertainment responded adequately. Taeyang's long silence during YG's broader 2019 crisis was also noted by commentators, though he was serving in the military at the time.

## Legacy & Influence

Taeyang helped legitimise R&B as a mainstream idol genre in South Korea and proved that solo work could coexist with group membership over the long term. Younger vocalists frequently cite him as a model for combining live vocal ability with dance performance. His marriage to actress Min Hyo-rin in February 2018 and his transition to The Black Label mark a second act that keeps him active in both Big Bang, whose 2022 single "Still Life" reunited the remaining members, and a solo career now nearing two decades.
