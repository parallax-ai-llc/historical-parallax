## Summary

Vladimir Guerrero Jr. is a first baseman for the Toronto Blue Jays and one of the most prominent hitters of his generation in Major League Baseball. Born in Montreal on March 16, 1999, while his father, Hall of Famer Vladimir Guerrero Sr., played for the Expos, he grew up in the Dominican Republic and signed with Toronto as a 16-year-old international free agent in 2015.

He arrived in the majors in 2019 as the consensus top prospect in baseball and delivered a breakout 2021 season, tying for the MLB lead with 48 home runs and finishing second in American League MVP voting. In April 2025 he signed a 14-year, $500 million extension, one of the largest contracts in the history of the sport, and that autumn he was named ALCS MVP as the Blue Jays reached their first World Series since 1993.

Guerrero matters beyond his statistics. He is the face of Canada's only MLB franchise, the first son of a Hall of Famer to become a star in his own right at this level, and a central figure in debates over service-time manipulation and franchise-player compensation.

## Early Life & Background

Guerrero was born in Montreal, Quebec, during his father's tenure with the Expos, which gave him Canadian citizenship by birth. He was raised primarily in Don Gregorio, a small town in the Dominican Republic's Peravia province, surrounded by a baseball family that included his uncle Wilton Guerrero, a former major league infielder.

He trained at the family's academy and drew scouts' attention for his advanced bat-to-ball skills and raw power. On July 2, 2015, the Blue Jays signed him for a $3.9 million bonus, the largest international signing in franchise history at the time.

## Rise to Prominence

Guerrero moved quickly through the minor leagues. In 2018 he hit .381 with 20 home runs across Double-A and Triple-A and was named Baseball America's Minor League Player of the Year. Toronto kept him in the minors to open 2019, citing defensive development, and he debuted on April 26, 2019, against Oakland. That July he hit a record 91 home runs in the Home Run Derby, losing the final to Pete Alonso.

After two uneven seasons split between third base and first base, Guerrero committed to first base and reshaped his conditioning before 2021. The result was a career year: a .311 average, 48 home runs, 111 RBI, and an AL-best 123 runs, .401 on-base percentage, .601 slugging percentage, and 1.002 OPS. He won the All-Star Game MVP, a Silver Slugger, and the Hank Aaron Award, and finished second to Shohei Ohtani in MVP voting.

## Key Achievements

- Five consecutive All-Star selections from 2021 to 2025.
- Tied for the MLB home run lead in 2021 with 48.
- AL Gold Glove at first base in 2022.
- 2023 Home Run Derby champion, making the Guerreros the first father-son pair to win the event.
- Silver Slugger in 2024 after hitting .323 with 30 home runs and 103 RBI.
- 14-year, $500 million contract extension signed in April 2025 with no deferred money.
- 2025 ALCS MVP, including a grand slam in the Division Series against the Yankees, as Toronto reached Game 7 of the World Series.

## Controversies & Criticism

The Blue Jays' decision to delay his 2019 debut was widely viewed as service-time manipulation and became a talking point in the 2022 collective bargaining negotiations. Early in his career, critics questioned his conditioning and defense, and his 2023 season, in which he hit .264 with 26 home runs, prompted concerns about consistency.

In 2023 he remarked that he would never sign with the Yankees, "not even dead," a comment that generated headlines in both markets. His salary arbitration hearing in 2024, which he won at $19.9 million, and the prolonged extension talks before the 2025 deadline he set drew scrutiny over whether the franchise could retain him.

## Legacy & Influence

Guerrero has become a symbol of the Blue Jays' rebuild and of baseball's growing Dominican-Canadian connection. His contract reset expectations for what a homegrown star can command, and his 2025 postseason established him as one of the sport's leading October performers.
