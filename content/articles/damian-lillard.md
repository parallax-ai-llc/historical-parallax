## Summary

Damian Lamonte Ollie Lillard Sr. (born July 15, 1990) is an American professional basketball player, a point guard best known for his eleven seasons with the Portland Trail Blazers. Selected sixth overall in the 2012 NBA draft out of Weber State, he was a unanimous Rookie of the Year and went on to become Portland's all-time leading scorer, a nine-time All-Star, and a member of the NBA 75th Anniversary Team.

Lillard's reputation rests on late-game shot-making. Two series-ending buzzer-beaters, against Houston in 2014 and Oklahoma City in 2019, gave rise to the "Dame Time" nickname. He is also one of the most prolific long-range shooters in league history and holds Portland's single-game record with 71 points.

After a contentious 2023 trade request sent him to the Milwaukee Bucks, a torn Achilles tendon in April 2025 and a subsequent contract release led to his return to Portland on a three-year deal in July 2025. Outside basketball, he records rap music under the name Dame D.O.L.L.A. and has a long-running signature shoe line with Adidas.

## Early Life & Background

Lillard was born in Oakland, California, and grew up in the Brookfield Village neighborhood of East Oakland. He attended Arroyo High School and St. Joseph Notre Dame before finishing at Oakland High School, where he averaged 22.4 points as a senior but drew little recruiting attention from major programs.

He enrolled at Weber State University in Ogden, Utah, in 2008. He was named Big Sky Conference Player of the Year in 2010 and again in 2012, after a broken foot cost him most of the 2010–11 season. As a senior in 2011–12 he averaged 24.5 points per game, second in Division I. Weber State retired his No. 1 jersey in 2015.

## Rise to Prominence

Portland drafted Lillard sixth overall in 2012. He averaged 19.0 points and 6.5 assists as a rookie, won the Skills Challenge, and became the fourth unanimous Rookie of the Year in league history.

On May 2, 2014, he hit a three-pointer with 0.9 seconds left in Game 6 to eliminate the Houston Rockets, Portland's first playoff series win since 2000. In 2019 he closed out Oklahoma City with a 37-foot shot at the buzzer in Game 5 and led the Trail Blazers to the Western Conference Finals, their deepest run since 2000. That summer he signed a four-year, $196 million supermax extension, followed by a further two-year, $122 million extension in 2022.

In September 2023, after requesting a trade, he was sent to Milwaukee in a three-team deal that moved Jrue Holiday to Portland. He made the All-Star team in both Bucks seasons but was diagnosed with deep vein thrombosis in March 2025 and tore his left Achilles on April 27, 2025, in the first round against Indiana. Milwaukee waived him in July 2025 using the stretch provision, and Portland signed him days later to a three-year, roughly $42 million contract including a no-trade clause. He sat out the 2025–26 season while rehabilitating.

## Key Achievements

- Rookie of the Year (2013); nine All-Star selections (2014–15, 2018–21, 2023–25)
- Eight All-NBA selections, including First Team in 2018
- All-Star Game MVP (2024) and back-to-back Three-Point Contest titles (2023, 2024)
- Olympic gold medal with the United States at the Tokyo Games in 2021
- NBA 75th Anniversary Team (2021)
- Career-high 71 points against Houston on February 26, 2023, with 13 three-pointers
- Portland franchise leader in points (more than 19,000) and three-pointers made
- Four rap albums as Dame D.O.L.L.A., beginning with "The Letter O" (2016)

## Controversies & Criticism

Lillard built his brand on loyalty to a small market, so his July 2023 trade request drew sharp criticism, particularly reports that his agent, Aaron Goodwin, warned teams other than the Miami Heat against acquiring him. The NBA circulated a memo to teams and the players' union cautioning that such conduct could bring discipline.

Critics have also questioned his defensive impact and pointed to Portland's limited playoff success during his tenure, with only one conference finals appearance. His 2025 return to Portland, including a no-trade clause for a player recovering from an Achilles tear, was debated as a sentimental rather than competitive signing. Earlier public disputes included exchanges of diss tracks with Shaquille O'Neal in 2019 and a feud with Paul George after the "bad shot" comment that followed his 2019 series-winner.

## Legacy & Influence

Lillard is widely regarded as the greatest player in Trail Blazers history alongside Clyde Drexler and Bill Walton, and his deep-range shooting helped normalize attempts from well beyond the arc. His path from an unheralded mid-major program to the NBA's 75th Anniversary Team is frequently cited as a model for overlooked recruits. His community work in Oakland and Portland, along with his music career, extended his influence beyond the court.
