## Summary

Felix (born Lee Yong-bok, 15 September 2000) is an Australian singer, rapper and dancer based in South Korea. He is a member of Stray Kids, the eight-member boy group managed by JYP Entertainment that debuted on 25 March 2018. Within the group he is known for an unusually deep bass voice that contrasts with his youthful appearance, and he handles rap, vocal and dance duties across the group's discography.

Felix matters beyond his musical role because he is one of the most visible Korean-Australian figures in global pop culture. His trajectory from a trainee who was eliminated on a survival programme to a Louis Vuitton house ambassador tracks the wider rise of Stray Kids, which became one of the first K-pop acts to place multiple consecutive albums at number one on the US Billboard 200.

## Early Life & Background

Felix was born and raised in Sydney, New South Wales, to South Korean parents. He grew up speaking English at home and in school, and his Korean was limited when he began his idol career. As a child and teenager he trained in taekwondo, competing at junior level, and he has cited that background as the foundation of his dance ability.

He was scouted at a JYP Entertainment audition held in Sydney and moved to Seoul in early 2017 to become a trainee. His training period was short by industry standards, roughly a year before his public debut.

## Rise to Prominence

In late 2017 JYP and Mnet aired the reality survival programme *Stray Kids* (17 October to 19 December 2017), which followed nine trainees led by Bang Chan as they attempted to debut as a self-producing group. Felix's limited Korean and stage inexperience drew criticism from the company's evaluators, and he was eliminated in the eighth episode alongside Lee Know. Both were reinstated in the finale, and the full nine-member line-up was confirmed.

Stray Kids released the pre-debut EP *Mixtape* on 8 January 2018 and officially debuted with *I Am Not* on 25 March 2018. The group became eight members after Woojin's departure in October 2019. Felix's low register became a signature hook in tracks such as "God's Menu" (2020), "Thunderous" (2021) and "S-Class" (2023).

## Key Achievements

- **Billboard 200 run:** Stray Kids debuted at number one with *Oddinary* (March 2022), *Maxident* (October 2022), *5-Star* (June 2023), *Rock-Star* (November 2023), *Ate* (July 2024), *Hop* (December 2024) and *Karma* (August 2025), an unprecedented streak for a Korean act.
- **Sales milestone:** *5-Star* passed five million copies in 2023, making Stray Kids the first act to reach that figure with a single album under the Circle Chart system.
- **Festival headlining:** the group headlined Lollapalooza Paris (2023), Lollapalooza Chicago (2024) and BST Hyde Park in London (2025), and won Best K-pop at the 2023 MTV Video Music Awards for "S-Class".
- **Solo and fashion work:** Felix released solo tracks including "Deep End" (2021) and "Unfair" (2022) through the group's SKZ-Record and SKZ-Replay projects, and was named a house ambassador for Louis Vuitton in April 2023.

## Controversies & Criticism

Felix's early career was shaped by online criticism of his Korean language ability during the 2017 survival show, which fed a wider debate about how foreign-born trainees are evaluated in the Korean idol system. As a Korean-Australian public figure he has also faced periodic disputes over identity and representation from both Korean and international audiences.

Stray Kids as a group has drawn criticism on fan-safety and privacy grounds, including repeated incidents involving intrusive "sasaeng" followers and a June 2023 traffic accident during a promotional schedule that injured three members and prompted questions about scheduling pressure. Felix himself has not been the subject of major individual scandals.

## Legacy & Influence

Felix's prominence has helped normalise non-Korean-born members as central rather than supplementary figures in K-pop groups, and he is frequently cited in Australian media as an example of the country's growing footprint in the industry. His distinctive vocal timbre has become a recognisable element of Stray Kids' self-produced sound, and his luxury-fashion partnerships reflect the industry's expanding overlap with global brands.
