## Summary

Alejandro González Iñárritu (born August 15, 1963) is a Mexican film director, screenwriter, and producer. He rose to international prominence with his 2000 debut *Amores perros* and became one of the most awarded filmmakers of the 2010s, winning consecutive Academy Awards for Best Director for *Birdman* (2014) and *The Revenant* (2015).

He was the first Mexican director to win the Best Director Oscar and the first filmmaker since Joseph L. Mankiewicz in 1950 to win it in back-to-back years. His five Academy Awards, including a Special Achievement Award for the virtual reality installation *Carne y Arena* (2017), make him among the most decorated directors of his generation.

Alongside Alfonso Cuarón and Guillermo del Toro, he is one of the "Three Amigos" of Mexican cinema whose success reshaped Hollywood's relationship with Latin American filmmakers. His work is known for interwoven narratives, moral weight, and technically ambitious long takes.

## Early Life & Background

Iñárritu was born in Mexico City, the youngest of seven children. His father, Héctor González Gama, was a banker whose business collapsed when Alejandro was young, leaving the family in modest circumstances in the Narvarte neighborhood. At 17, he worked on cargo ships, traveling through Europe and North Africa, an experience he has cited as formative.

He studied communications at the Universidad Iberoamericana. In 1984 he began hosting at Mexico City rock station WFM, becoming its director by his early twenties. He later composed music for Mexican films and directed television commercials, founding the production company Zeta Films in 1991.

## Rise to Prominence

His feature debut *Amores perros* (2000), written by Guillermo Arriaga, won the Critics' Week Grand Prize at Cannes and earned an Oscar nomination for Best Foreign Language Film. Its three-strand structure established the style he continued in *21 Grams* (2003), starring Sean Penn, Naomi Watts, and Benicio del Toro.

*Babel* (2006) won him the Best Director prize at Cannes and received seven Academy Award nominations, winning for Gustavo Santaolalla's score. It also won the Golden Globe for Best Motion Picture – Drama. *Biutiful* (2010) earned Javier Bardem the Best Actor award at Cannes and two Oscar nominations.

## Key Achievements

*Birdman or (The Unexpected Virtue of Ignorance)* (2014), shot by Emmanuel Lubezki to appear as a single continuous take, won four Oscars: Best Picture, Best Director, Best Original Screenplay, and Best Cinematography. *The Revenant* (2015), starring Leonardo DiCaprio, received 12 nominations and won three, including DiCaprio's first Best Actor award, and grossed over $530 million worldwide.

In 2017 *Carne y Arena*, a VR installation about migrants crossing the US–Mexico border, premiered at Cannes and received a Special Achievement Academy Award, the first such award since 1996. In 2019 he became the first Mexican to preside over the Cannes Film Festival jury. *Bardo, False Chronicle of a Handful of Truths* (2022) premiered in competition at Venice and was Oscar-nominated for Darius Khondji's cinematography.

## Controversies & Criticism

His collaboration with Arriaga ended acrimoniously after *Babel*, with a public dispute over authorship of the trilogy. The production of *The Revenant* drew reports of a "living hell" shoot, with crew departures and a budget that reportedly grew to around $135 million, though Iñárritu defended his methods. His 2014 description of superhero films as "cultural genocide" provoked wide debate, and *Bardo* was criticized by some reviewers as self-indulgent, prompting Iñárritu to publicly challenge critics.

## Legacy & Influence

Iñárritu's awards run helped make Mexican directors a dominant force at the Academy Awards between 2013 and 2019. His hyperlink narratives influenced a wave of ensemble dramas, while *Birdman* and *The Revenant* pushed long-take and natural-light techniques into mainstream prestige filmmaking. His later work, including a film starring Tom Cruise shot in 2025, continues to attract major studio backing.
