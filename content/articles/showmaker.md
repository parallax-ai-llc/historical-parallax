## Summary

Heo Su, known competitively as ShowMaker, is a South Korean professional League of Legends player who has spent his entire top-level career as the mid laner for Dplus KIA, the organization formerly known as DAMWON Gaming and DWG KIA. Born on 22 July 2000, he joined the team in late 2017 and debuted in Korea's top league, the LCK, in 2019.

ShowMaker is best known as the mid laner of the 2020 World Championship–winning roster, which ended a two-year drought for Korean teams at the tournament. He was named the LCK's most valuable player for both the 2020 Summer and 2021 Spring splits and is widely regarded as one of the most mechanically gifted mid laners of his generation, with a champion pool spanning control mages, assassins and unconventional picks.

His importance lies in anchoring one of the LCK's defining dynasties of the early 2020s and in his loyalty to a single organization at a time when roster turnover became the norm in Korean esports.

## Early Life & Background

Heo Su was born in South Korea on 22 July 2000. Like many Korean professionals, he came to attention through the solo-queue ladder, reaching the top ranks of the Korean server while still a teenager. He joined DAMWON Gaming as a trainee in 2017, when the organization competed in Challengers Korea, the second tier of Korean competition. His handle, ShowMaker, was chosen to reflect a playstyle built around aggressive, highlight-generating plays rather than passive lane control.

## Rise to Prominence

ShowMaker became DAMWON's starting mid laner in 2018. The team won Challengers Korea in 2018 and earned promotion to the LCK for the 2019 season. In its rookie LCK year, DAMWON qualified for the 2019 World Championship and reached the quarterfinals, losing to G2 Esports.

The breakthrough came in 2020. DAMWON went 16–2 in the LCK Summer regular season, swept DRX 3–0 in the final, and ShowMaker was named regular-season MVP. At the 2020 World Championship in Shanghai, the team defeated Suning 3–1 in the final to become world champions. ShowMaker's Twisted Fate and Zoe play were central to the run.

In 2021 the rebranded DWG KIA won both LCK splits, beating Gen.G 3–0 in Spring and T1 3–1 in Summer. ShowMaker was Spring MVP and Spring Finals MVP. The team finished runner-up at the 2021 Mid-Season Invitational, losing 2–3 to Royal Never Give Up, and runner-up at the 2021 World Championship, falling 2–3 to Edward Gaming in the final.

## Key Achievements

- **World Champion**, 2020, with DAMWON Gaming.
- **LCK champion** three times: 2020 Summer, 2021 Spring, 2021 Summer.
- **LCK regular-season MVP**: 2020 Summer and 2021 Spring.
- **Worlds finalist** in consecutive years, 2020 and 2021.
- **Six consecutive World Championship appearances** from 2019 through 2024.
- One of a small group of players to surpass 1,000 career kills in the LCK.

## Controversies & Criticism

ShowMaker's career has been largely free of personal scandal. Criticism has instead focused on performance. After 2021, DK's results declined: the team exited the 2022 Worlds in the quarterfinals and was eliminated in the Swiss stage in both 2023 and 2024. Some analysts argued that ShowMaker's laning fell behind rivals such as Chovy and Faker, and that his willingness to take early risks had become a liability on weaker rosters. Supporters counter that roster instability after jungler Canyon's departure to Gen.G ahead of 2024 explains much of the drop. His decision to keep re-signing with Dplus KIA rather than seek a stronger team has also been debated by fans.

## Legacy & Influence

ShowMaker's 2020 title made DAMWON the first team outside the T1, Samsung and Gen.G lineage to win Worlds for Korea, broadening the LCK's competitive landscape. He popularized aggressive mid-lane picks such as Zoe, Syndra and Sylas at the highest level and was among the first players of his era to be framed as a successor to Faker. His long tenure with one organization has made him the face of Dplus KIA and a reference point for loyalty in a transfer-heavy industry.
