## Summary

Patrick Lavon Mahomes II (born September 17, 1995) is an American football quarterback for the Kansas City Chiefs of the National Football League (NFL). Since becoming the Chiefs' starter in 2018, he has won three Super Bowls (LIV, LVII, LVIII), three Super Bowl MVP awards, and two NFL Most Valuable Player awards (2018, 2022), establishing himself as the defining quarterback of his era.

Mahomes is known for an improvisational, off-platform throwing style, exceptional arm strength, and late-game comebacks. Under his leadership the Chiefs reached seven consecutive AFC Championship Games (2018–2024 seasons) and became the first team in two decades to win back-to-back Super Bowls (2022 and 2023 seasons). His ten-year, $450 million contract extension signed in July 2020 was, at the time, the largest in the history of professional sports.

## Early Life & Background

Mahomes was born in Tyler, Texas, to Pat Mahomes, a Major League Baseball pitcher who played for six teams between 1992 and 2003, and Randi Martin. He grew up around professional clubhouses and attended Whitehouse High School in Whitehouse, Texas, where he starred in football, basketball, and baseball. As a senior he threw for 4,619 yards and 50 touchdowns.

The Detroit Tigers selected him in the 37th round of the 2014 MLB Draft, but he chose to play football at Texas Tech University. In 2016, under coach Kliff Kingsbury's Air Raid offense, he led the Football Bowl Subdivision with 5,052 passing yards and 53 total touchdowns, and set an NCAA single-game record with 734 yards of total offense against Oklahoma on October 22, 2016.

## Rise to Prominence

The Chiefs traded up from the 27th pick to select Mahomes 10th overall in the 2017 NFL Draft. He spent his rookie year behind Alex Smith, starting one game in Week 17. After Smith was traded, Mahomes became the starter in 2018 and threw for 5,097 yards and 50 touchdowns, becoming only the second player after Peyton Manning to reach both marks in a single season. He was named NFL MVP and Offensive Player of the Year at age 23.

In his second season as a starter he led Kansas City to a 31–20 win over the San Francisco 49ers in Super Bowl LIV on February 2, 2020, the franchise's first title in 50 years, and was named the game's MVP at age 24. He signed his landmark extension five months later.

## Key Achievements

- Super Bowl champion and MVP three times: LIV (2020), LVII (2023, 38–35 over Philadelphia), and LVIII (2024, 25–22 in overtime over San Francisco).
- NFL MVP in 2018 and 2022; first-team All-Pro both seasons.
- Fastest player in NFL history to reach several passing milestones, including 20,000 and 25,000 career yards.
- Chiefs played in five Super Bowls in six seasons (2019–2024), losing LV (2021) and LIX (2025).
- Founded the 15 and the Mahomies Foundation in 2019 and holds minority ownership stakes in the Kansas City Royals, Sporting Kansas City, and the Kansas City Current.

## Controversies & Criticism

Mahomes has drawn criticism for sideline conduct, most notably in December 2023 when he berated officials and threw his helmet after a late offside penalty against the Buffalo Bills; the NFL fined him $50,000. As the Chiefs' dominance grew, a recurring narrative among fans and commentators alleged favorable officiating, a claim Mahomes and the league have rejected. His younger brother, Jackson Mahomes, faced criminal charges in 2023, and family matters, including his wife Brittany's political social media activity in 2024, have periodically drawn scrutiny that he has said he keeps separate from his career. On December 14, 2025, he tore his ACL against the Los Angeles Chargers, ending a season in which Kansas City missed the playoffs for the first time in his tenure as starter.

## Legacy & Influence

Mahomes redefined expectations for young quarterbacks, winning an MVP and a Super Bowl within his first two seasons as a starter. His no-look and sidearm throws, popularized on national broadcasts, influenced a generation of college and high school passers, while his contract structure reshaped NFL quarterback negotiations. Frequently compared with Tom Brady, he is widely regarded as the leading candidate to be considered the greatest quarterback of the 2020s.
