## Summary

Christopher Alesund, known competitively as GeT_RiGhT, is a Swedish former professional Counter-Strike player born on 29 May 1990. He competed at the top level of both Counter-Strike 1.6 and Counter-Strike: Global Offensive for more than a decade, most famously as the lurker of Ninjas in Pyjamas (NiP) from 2012 to 2019.

He is widely regarded as one of the best players in the history of the game. HLTV.org ranked him the world's number one player in both 2013 and 2014, and he was a core member of the NiP lineup that recorded an 87-map winning streak on LAN at the start of CS:GO's competitive life. He later won the ESL One Cologne 2014 Major with the same team.

Beyond results, GeT_RiGhT helped define the lurker role and became one of the most recognisable faces of the Swedish esports scene during its most dominant period.

## Early Life & Background

Alesund was born in Stockholm, Sweden, in 1990 and grew up playing Counter-Strike in the country's dense LAN and internet café culture of the early 2000s. He joined organised teams as a teenager, competing for Begrip Gaming around 2007 before moving through the Swedish 1.6 circuit.

He has spoken publicly about living with a chronic intestinal illness, which affected his health and travel schedule at points in his career, and about the stress of maintaining top form across many years of competition.

## Rise to Prominence

GeT_RiGhT reached the elite tier of Counter-Strike 1.6 with SK Gaming in 2008 and then Fnatic, which he joined in 2009. With Fnatic he won the Intel Extreme Masters IV World Championship in 2010 and several other international events, and he was regularly placed among HLTV's top 20 players of the year.

After a return to SK Gaming in 2011, he joined the newly re-formed Ninjas in Pyjamas in August 2012 alongside f0rest, friberg, Xizt and Fifflaren. The team switched to the freshly released CS:GO and immediately dominated, winning 87 consecutive maps on LAN between late 2012 and April 2013, a record that has never been repeated.

## Key Achievements

- **87-0 LAN map streak** with NiP from 2012 to 2013, the longest in CS:GO history.
- **ESL One Cologne 2014 Major champion**, NiP's only Major title, achieved after three consecutive Major final losses.
- **HLTV world number one** in 2013 and 2014, and a top-20 placement in numerous other years across two game versions.
- **IEM IV World Championship 2010** winner with Fnatic in Counter-Strike 1.6.
- **Four Major finals** with NiP between 2013 and 2015, including runner-up finishes at DreamHack Winter 2013, EMS One Katowice 2014 and ESL One Katowice 2015.

## Controversies & Criticism

GeT_RiGhT's career was largely free of scandal, but his later years drew criticism. From roughly 2016 onward his statistical output declined, and NiP's results fell well below the standard of the 2012 to 2015 era. Fans and analysts questioned whether the organisation kept him for marketing reasons rather than performance.

In September 2019 NiP moved him to the bench, ending a seven-year run. He later criticised how the transition was handled and spoke about feeling disrespected by the organisation. He moved to Dignitas in January 2020, reuniting with f0rest, friberg and Xizt, but the project failed to return to the top tier and he was benched again in 2021 before stepping away from professional play.

## Legacy & Influence

GeT_RiGhT is credited with popularising the lurker role, in which a player operates away from the team to gather information and punish rotations. His ability to win clutch situations and read opponents made the role a fixture in team structures worldwide.

Alongside f0rest, he formed one of the longest-lasting duos in esports history, spanning roughly a decade and two games. NiP's early CS:GO dominance is often cited as a key factor in the game's rapid growth as a spectator sport. After retiring from competition he moved into full-time streaming and content creation, remaining a prominent public figure in the Counter-Strike community.
