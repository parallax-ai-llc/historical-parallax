## Summary

The August 2025 Indonesian protests began on 25 August as student demonstrations against a new housing allowance for members of the House of Representatives (DPR). They grew into nationwide unrest after police killed a delivery driver [^1]. Rioters burned regional parliament buildings and looted the homes of several lawmakers and of Finance Minister Sri Mulyani Indrawati [^2]. Prabowo Subianto revoked the perks and replaced five ministers, including Sri Mulyani, which made the unrest the biggest domestic challenge of his presidency so far [^3].

## Background

Lawmakers were receiving a monthly housing allowance of 50 million rupiah (about $3,075), close to ten times Jakarta's minimum wage [^3]. The payments came at a time of rising living costs, layoffs, and government budget cuts. A viral video, later described as a hoax, appeared to show lawmakers mocking public criticism, which added to the anger [^1]. Earlier in 2025, students had already protested under the "Indonesia Gelap" ("Dark Indonesia") banner over budget cuts and a revision of the armed forces law [^4].

## The Event

Students rallied outside the parliament complex in Jakarta on 25 August, and protests soon spread to Medan and other cities [^1]. On 28 August, during clashes near parliament, a police Mobile Brigade (Brimob) armored vehicle ran over and killed 21-year-old app-based motorcycle taxi driver Affan Kurniawan. Video of his death fueled mass outrage [^2][^3]. Over the following days, protesters set fire to regional council buildings. In Makassar, three people died when the city's parliament building was set ablaze [^2]. On 30-31 August, crowds stormed and looted the homes of lawmakers Ahmad Sahroni, Eko Patrio, and Uya Kuya, as well as Sri Mulyani's house [^2]. Protest leaders then published the "17+8 demands": 17 short-term demands and 8 longer-term reforms, covering accountability for police violence, cutting lawmakers' perks, and police and military reform [^1].

## Response and Aftermath

On 31 August, Prabowo announced that the DPR allowance would be revoked and overseas work trips for lawmakers suspended. He also warned that security forces would act firmly against looting [^2]. He cancelled a planned trip to China [^1]. Five DPR members were suspended by their parties, and the officer responsible for Affan's death was dishonorably discharged [^1]. On 8 September, Prabowo replaced five ministers. Economist Purbaya Yudhi Sadewa succeeded Sri Mulyani as finance minister, and coordinating security minister Budi Gunawan was also removed [^3]. Death toll counts differ. Indonesia's National Commission on Human Rights documented 10 deaths and the police reported 7 [^3].

## Positive Perspectives

- The government gave in to the central demand within days and revoked the housing allowance [^2].
- A police officer involved in Affan Kurniawan's death was dishonorably discharged, and Mobile Brigade officers were detained for investigation [^1].
- The 17+8 demands gave a loosely organized movement a shared, specific reform agenda [^1].
- The protests showed young Indonesians and gig workers acting as a political force, with solidarity rallies held abroad [^1].
- Al Jazeera reported that the protests subsided after the allowances were revoked [^3].

## Negative Perspectives

- Police violence, especially the killing of Affan Kurniawan, led to accusations that security forces used excessive force [^2].
- Arson and looting caused deaths, including three people in the Makassar parliament fire [^2].
- The death toll is disputed. The human rights commission's figure is higher than the police count [^3].
- The unrest was set off by a hoax video as well as by real grievances, which shows how fast misinformation can inflame a tense situation [^1].
- Many of the longer-term demands, such as police reform, were left as proposals rather than enacted changes [^1].

## Recent News

Wikipedia treats the August unrest as part of a wider wave of protests against government policy that continued into 2026. No final official accounting of the August 2025 deaths could be verified [^4].
