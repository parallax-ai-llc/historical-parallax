## Summary

Jihyo (born Park Ji-soo on 1 February 1997, later legally renamed Park Ji-hyo) is a South Korean singer and the leader of the girl group Twice, managed by JYP Entertainment. She is the group's main vocalist and one of the longest-serving trainees in the company's history, having spent roughly ten years preparing before her debut in October 2015.

She matters both as the anchoring voice of one of the best-selling K-pop girl groups of the 2010s and 2020s and as a solo artist in her own right. Her first solo EP, *Zone* (2023), entered the top ten of the US Billboard 200, a rare feat for a Korean female soloist. Her long trainee period and her public discussion of anxiety have also made her a reference point in conversations about the demands of the idol training system.

## Early Life & Background

Jihyo was born in Guri, Gyeonggi Province, South Korea, the eldest of three daughters. In 2005, at the age of eight, she placed second in a "Best Kid Face" contest run by the Junior Naver portal, which led to an offer from JYP Entertainment. She joined the company that year and trained for about a decade. During this period she made minor appearances as a child actress and was periodically expected to debut in groups that never launched, including a planned six-member team in 2013 that was cancelled. She attended Daekyeong Commercial High School in Seoul, later graduating from Hanlim Multi Art School.

## Rise to Prominence

In 2015 Jihyo competed in *Sixteen*, a Mnet survival show in which 16 JYP trainees vied for spots in a new girl group. She was selected as a member of Twice and, by a vote of her fellow members, named the group's leader. Twice debuted on 20 October 2015 with the EP *The Story Begins* and the single "Like Ooh-Ahh". The group's breakthrough came in 2016 with "Cheer Up" and "TT", which turned Twice into one of the dominant acts in Korea and later Japan. Jihyo's role as main vocalist and spokesperson became central to the group's image as it expanded into stadium tours across Asia and North America. In July 2022, all nine members renewed their contracts with JYP Entertainment.

## Key Achievements

- **Twice discography:** As leader and main vocalist, Jihyo has been part of more than a dozen Korean-language EPs and albums, numerous Japanese releases, and multiple Daesang (grand prize) wins at Korean year-end award shows.
- **Solo debut:** *Zone*, released on 18 August 2023 with the lead single "Killin' Me Good", sold over 400,000 copies in its first week according to Hanteo and debuted at number 8 on the Billboard 200.
- **Songwriting:** She has lyric credits on Twice tracks including "Eye Eye Eyes" (2017), and contributed writing to several songs on *Zone*.
- **Live milestones:** She led Twice through Japanese dome tours and the group's 2023 stadium shows in the United States, including a sold-out concert at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles.

## Controversies & Criticism

In August 2019, Jihyo's relationship with singer Kang Daniel was confirmed by both agencies, prompting hostility from some fans and a formal warning from JYP against online harassment. The couple confirmed their separation in November 2020. Also in 2020, she paused activities for several weeks due to anxiety, which drew both sympathy and debate about idol workloads. Some critics have questioned whether Twice's tightly controlled concept left limited room for her vocal range, a criticism partly addressed by her solo work.

## Legacy & Influence

Jihyo's decade-long trainee period is frequently cited as an example of the persistence the K-pop system demands, and her eventual success has been used by trainees and commentators as a counterpoint to the industry's preference for rapid debuts. As leader, she is credited with the group's public stability through contract renewals and lineup pressures that broke up many contemporaries. Her solo chart performance helped establish that members of established girl groups could launch commercially viable solo careers without leaving their groups.
