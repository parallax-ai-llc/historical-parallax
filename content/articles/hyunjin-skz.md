## Summary

Hyunjin, born Hwang Hyun-jin on 20 March 2000 in Seoul, is a South Korean singer, rapper, and dancer best known as a member of the boy group Stray Kids under JYP Entertainment. Within the group he serves as a main dancer and rapper, and he has become one of its most visible members through fashion partnerships, solo tracks, and choreography work.

Stray Kids debuted in March 2018 and grew from a self-producing rookie act into one of the most commercially successful K-pop groups of the 2020s, with a string of consecutive number-one albums on the US Billboard 200. Hyunjin's stage presence and dance ability are frequently cited as central to the group's performance-driven identity.

His career has not been free of difficulty. A 2021 bullying allegation led to a months-long hiatus, after which he returned to full activity. He has since expanded into luxury fashion as a global ambassador and into songwriting and painting.

## Early Life & Background

Hyunjin was born in Seoul and grew up in the Gangdong District. As a child he spent roughly a year living in Las Vegas in the United States before returning to South Korea. He attended the School of Performing Arts Seoul, a high school known for producing entertainers.

In 2015 he was street-cast by a JYP Entertainment scout, an unusual entry point since he had no prior formal dance training. He entered the company as a trainee and spent about two years preparing for debut, developing dance skills that later became his defining trait.

## Rise to Prominence

In late 2017 Hyunjin appeared on the Mnet survival programme *Stray Kids*, in which leader Bang Chan assembled a team of JYP trainees. The group released the pre-debut EP *Mixtape* in January 2018 and officially debuted on 25 March 2018 with *I Am Not*.

Stray Kids built a reputation for self-produced music led by the in-house team 3Racha. Hyunjin's role grew steadily: he formed the dance-focused sub-unit DanceRacha with Lee Know and Felix, contributed choreography, and appeared in a duet with Bang Chan, "Red Lights", on the 2021 album *Noeasy*. Solo releases through the group's SKZ-Player and SKZ-Record series, including "Play With Fire" and "Little Star", established his individual voice.

In September 2023 Versace named him a global brand ambassador, and in May 2024 he attended the Met Gala with Stray Kids, the first K-pop group to appear there as a full unit.

## Key Achievements

- **Billboard 200 dominance**: Stray Kids reached number one with *Oddinary* (2022), *Maxident* (2022), *5-Star* (2023), *Rock-Star* (2023), *Ate* (2024), *Hop* (2024), and *Karma* (2025), a run of seven consecutive chart-toppers.
- **Sales**: *5-Star* sold more than 5 million copies, among the largest first-week totals in K-pop history.
- **Songwriting**: Co-writing credits include "Red Lights", "Play With Fire", and "Little Star".
- **Fashion**: Global ambassador for Versace since 2023.

## Controversies & Criticism

In February 2021 anonymous online posts accused Hyunjin of verbal bullying during his middle-school years. JYP Entertainment initially denied the claims, then announced it had met with those who raised the allegations. Hyunjin issued a handwritten apology on 26 February 2021 and suspended all activities. He returned on 26 June 2021, four months later. The incident drew criticism of both the artist and the company's handling, and it remains a reference point in debates about accountability in the industry.

## Legacy & Influence

Hyunjin is widely regarded as one of the leading dancers of the fourth generation of K-pop, and his performances have influenced the choreography-heavy style of many later groups. His crossover into high fashion helped normalise luxury-house ambassadorships for male idols outside the largest agencies. As a painter and lyricist, he has also expanded the range of creative outlets associated with K-pop performers.
