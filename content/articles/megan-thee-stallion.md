## Summary

Megan Thee Stallion, born Megan Jovon Ruth Pete on February 15, 1995, is an American rapper, singer, and songwriter from Houston, Texas. She rose from viral freestyle videos in the mid-2010s to become one of the defining rap artists of the 2020s, known for her confident delivery, explicit lyricism, and the "Hot Girl" persona that entered mainstream vocabulary.

Her commercial peak arrived in 2020, when the "Savage" remix with Beyoncé and "WAP" with Cardi B both topped the Billboard Hot 100. She won three Grammy Awards in 2021, including Best New Artist. She later became one of the few major rap stars to release music independently after a protracted legal dispute with her first label.

Her career has also been shaped by public trauma, most notably the July 2020 shooting for which fellow rapper Tory Lanez was convicted. Her openness about that event, and about mental health more broadly, has made her a significant cultural figure beyond music.

## Early Life & Background

Megan Pete was born in San Antonio, Texas, and raised primarily in Houston. Her mother, Holly Thomas, rapped under the name Holly-Wood and often brought her daughter to recording sessions. Her father died when she was 15.

She attended Pearland High School and began writing rhymes as a teenager. She enrolled at Prairie View A&M University before transferring to Texas Southern University, where she completed a Bachelor of Science in health administration in December 2021 while already a major star. Her mother, who served as her manager, died of a brain tumor in March 2019; her grandmother died the same month.

## Rise to Prominence

Freestyle clips filmed on the Texas Southern campus circulated widely around 2016. She released the mixtape "Rich Ratchet" that year and the EP "Make It Hot" in 2017. In 2018 she signed with the Houston independent label 1501 Certified Entertainment and released "Tina Snow", then became the first female rapper signed to 300 Entertainment.

The 2019 mixtape "Fever" and the single "Hot Girl Summer" with Nicki Minaj and Ty Dolla $ign made her a national name. Her 2020 EP "Suga" produced "Savage", whose Beyoncé remix hit number one in May 2020. "WAP" debuted at number one that August. Her debut studio album "Good News" followed on November 20, 2020.

## Key Achievements

- **Grammy Awards:** Best New Artist, Best Rap Performance, and Best Rap Song at the 2021 ceremony, all tied to the "Savage" remix.
- **Chart records:** Three Billboard Hot 100 number ones, including "Hiss" in February 2024, her first solo chart-topper.
- **Albums:** "Good News" (2020), "Traumazine" (2022), and "Megan" (2024), the last released through her own Hot Girl Productions.
- **Recognition:** Named to the Time 100 in 2020 and featured on the cover of the 2021 Sports Illustrated Swimsuit Issue.
- **Beyond music:** Hosted and performed on Saturday Night Live in October 2022, appeared in "She-Hulk" and "Dicks: The Musical", and founded the Pete and Thomas Foundation in 2022.

## Controversies & Criticism

On July 12, 2020, she was shot in both feet after a party in the Hollywood Hills. Tory Lanez was convicted on three felony counts in December 2022 and sentenced to 10 years in prison in August 2023. She faced sustained online harassment and disbelief during the case, which she has described as deeply damaging.

"WAP" drew criticism from conservative commentators over its sexual content, while supporters framed it as a statement of female autonomy. Her contract lawsuit against 1501 Certified Entertainment, filed in 2020, produced years of litigation before a settlement in October 2023. A public feud with Nicki Minaj erupted in 2024 after "Hiss" referenced Megan's Law. In 2024 a former cameraman filed a harassment lawsuit against her, which she denied.

## Legacy & Influence

Megan Thee Stallion helped make "Hot Girl Summer" a cultural shorthand and demonstrated that a female rapper could dominate charts with unapologetically sexual material. Her decision to release "Megan" independently is cited as a model for artist ownership. Her advocacy on mental health, including the Bad Bitches Have Bad Days Too resource site, has broadened public conversation among her fans.
