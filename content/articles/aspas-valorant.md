## Summary

Erick Santos, known competitively as aspas, is a Brazilian professional Valorant player born on February 20, 2002. He plays the duelist role, specializing in aggressive entry agents such as Jett and Raze, and is widely regarded as one of the most mechanically gifted players the game has produced.

Aspas rose to global fame as the star of LOUD, the Brazilian organization that won Valorant Champions 2022 in Istanbul, the first world title for a team from Latin America. He followed that with a dominant 2023 season in which LOUD won the inaugural VCT Americas league and reached the grand finals of two international events.

Beyond trophies, aspas matters because he redefined expectations for the duelist role. His consistent first-blood rates, high average combat scores and ability to win rounds alone made him a benchmark against which other entry players are measured.

## Early Life & Background

Aspas was born in Brazil in 2002 and grew up playing competitive first-person shooters on PC. Like many Brazilian players of his generation, he came into Valorant when the game entered closed beta in 2020, having previously played tactical shooters at an amateur level.

He built his reputation through Brazil's grassroots Valorant scene during 2020 and 2021, competing for smaller domestic organizations before catching the attention of larger teams. His in-game name comes from the Portuguese word for quotation marks.

## Rise to Prominence

In late 2021, LOUD assembled a new roster around aspas, Saadhak, Sacy, pANcada and Less. The lineup won the Brazilian qualifiers and reached the grand final of VCT Masters Reykjavík in April 2022, losing to OpTic Gaming.

The breakthrough came in September 2022 at Valorant Champions in Istanbul. LOUD defeated OpTic Gaming 3–1 in the final on September 18, 2022, becoming world champions. Aspas was among the tournament's statistical leaders and was widely singled out as its standout player.

In 2023, with a partially rebuilt roster, LOUD reached the final of VCT LOCK//IN São Paulo in March, losing 3–2 to Fnatic. LOUD then won the VCT Americas league in June 2023 and reached the Masters Tokyo final, again losing to Fnatic.

## Key Achievements

- Valorant Champions 2022 winner with LOUD, September 2022.
- VCT Americas 2023 league champion; named the league's most valuable player.
- Grand finalist at Masters Reykjavík 2022, LOCK//IN 2023 and Masters Tokyo 2023.
- Frequently ranked the number-one player in the world by community and analyst year-end lists for 2023.
- Joined Leviatán for the 2024 season and MIBR ahead of 2025, remaining one of the highest-rated duelists in the Americas league in both years.

## Controversies & Criticism

Aspas's departure from LOUD after Champions 2023 was contentious. Reports of a stalled contract negotiation and a large buyout fee fueled public debate in Brazil, with some fans blaming the player and others the organization. He signed with Leviatán in December 2023.

Critics have also argued that his style depends on teammates sacrificing their own impact to feed him resources, and that his individual numbers have not always translated into international titles after 2022. Supporters counter that his round-winning statistics hold up across three different rosters.

## Legacy & Influence

Aspas is one of the defining players of Valorant's first competitive era. The 2022 Champions win established Brazil as a top region and proved that a Latin American roster could beat European and North American favorites.

His approach to the duelist role, combining precise aim with patient timing rather than pure aggression, has been studied and copied by younger players. He remains a central figure in the Americas league and a reference point for what a franchise player looks like in Valorant.
