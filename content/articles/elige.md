## Summary

Jonathan "EliGE" Jablonowski (born April 16, 1997) is an American professional Counter-Strike player best known for his nearly nine-year tenure with Team Liquid, where he became one of the most consistent riflers in the history of North American Counter-Strike. He joined Liquid in January 2015 and remained on the roster until late 2023, an unusually long run in a scene where lineups turn over every few months.

EliGE was a core member of the 2019 Team Liquid squad that won the Intel Grand Slam Season 2 and held the world number one ranking, the first North American team to reach that position in the CS:GO era. Individually, he was named among HLTV's top 20 players of the year on multiple occasions, peaking at fourth in 2019. Since December 2023 he has competed for Complexity Gaming in Counter-Strike 2.

## Early Life & Background

Jablonowski was raised in a Polish-American family in the United States and speaks Polish. He began playing Counter-Strike as a teenager and rose through the North American online scene while still in high school. He has spoken about weighing a university path in computer science against esports and ultimately committing to competition full-time at 17, shortly before his first major professional contract.

## Rise to Prominence

After stints on semi-professional North American rosters in 2014, EliGE signed with Team Liquid in January 2015 alongside players such as nitr0, Hiko, and adreN. In 2016, with the addition of s1mple as a stand-in, Liquid reached the semifinals of MLG Columbus and then the grand final of ESL One Cologne, becoming the first North American team to play a CS:GO Major final. They lost that final 2–0 to SK Gaming.

Liquid's breakout came in 2019. With a lineup of EliGE, nitr0, NAF, Twistzz, and Stewie2K, the team won IEM Sydney, DreamHack Masters Dallas, ESL Pro League Season 9, and ESL One Cologne in roughly two months, securing the $1,000,000 Intel Grand Slam in record time. The team was ranked first in the world for much of the year, and EliGE finished fourth in HLTV's annual player ranking.

## Key Achievements

- **Intel Grand Slam Season 2 (2019)** with Team Liquid, worth $1,000,000.
- **First North American Major finalist** as part of Liquid at ESL One Cologne 2016.
- **HLTV Top 20** placements including fourth in 2019, one of the highest rankings ever earned by a North American player.
- **Longest tenure in Team Liquid history**, from January 2015 to December 2023.
- Career prize earnings exceeding $1 million across CS:GO and Counter-Strike 2.

EliGE's reputation rests on mechanical rifling, aggressive entry play, and durability: he was frequently Liquid's top-rated player across changing lineups and in-game leaders.

## Controversies & Criticism

EliGE is one of the more outspoken figures in the scene. He has repeatedly criticized Valve over cheating, matchmaking, and technical issues in Counter-Strike 2, and has publicly disputed with other players and commentators on social media. Critics have argued that his blunt public commentary occasionally added tension to already unstable rosters.

His departure from Team Liquid in late 2023 followed reported internal disagreements over roster direction and leadership during a period of underperformance. Liquid benched him and nitr0 in November 2023, and he joined Complexity Gaming the following month. Some fans questioned the move as a step down, while others viewed it as a reset for a player whose team had stagnated since 2019.

## Legacy & Influence

EliGE is widely regarded as one of the best players North America has produced, alongside contemporaries such as Twistzz and NAF. His 2019 season demonstrated that a North American core could reach and hold the top of the world ranking, a benchmark that had eluded the region for years. His longevity at Liquid made him a symbol of the organization's Counter-Strike identity, and his consistency across nearly a decade continues to be cited as a standard for rifling in the region.
