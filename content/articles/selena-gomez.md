## Summary

Selena Gomez is an American singer, actress, producer, and entrepreneur who moved from Disney Channel stardom to a multi-decade career spanning pop music, prestige television, film, and beauty. She first reached a mass audience as Alex Russo on *Wizards of Waverly Place* (2007–2012), then built a recording career with the band Selena Gomez & the Scene and as a solo artist, with three consecutive number-one albums on the Billboard 200.

Her later career is defined by range. She stars in and executive-produces the Hulu series *Only Murders in the Building* (2021–present), earned a shared Best Actress prize at the 2024 Cannes Film Festival for *Emilia Pérez*, and founded Rare Beauty in 2020, which led Bloomberg to estimate her net worth at roughly $1.3 billion in September 2024. She has also become one of the most visible public figures to speak openly about lupus, a kidney transplant, and bipolar disorder.

## Early Life & Background

Gomez was born on July 22, 1992, in Grand Prairie, Texas, and was named after Tejano singer Selena Quintanilla. Her mother, Mandy Teefey, was 16 at the time of her birth; her parents divorced when she was five. She was raised largely by her mother in modest circumstances, and later described the family's early financial strain. Her first screen role came at age 10 on *Barney & Friends* (2002–2004), which led to Disney Channel guest spots and a move to Los Angeles. She completed high school through homeschooling.

## Rise to Prominence

*Wizards of Waverly Place* premiered in October 2007 and ran for four seasons, winning two Primetime Emmys for Outstanding Children's Program. Disney films followed, including *Princess Protection Program* (2009) and *Ramona and Beezus* (2010). In 2009 she formed Selena Gomez & the Scene, releasing *Kiss & Tell* (2009), *A Year Without Rain* (2010), and *When the Sun Goes Down* (2011), which yielded the hit "Love You Like a Love Song." At 17 she became UNICEF's youngest ambassador at the time.

Her adult transition began with Harmony Korine's *Spring Breakers* (2012) and the solo album *Stars Dance* (2013), which debuted at number one. *Revival* (2015) produced "Good for You," "Same Old Love," and "Hands to Myself."

## Key Achievements

- **Music:** "Lose You to Love Me" (2019) became her first Billboard Hot 100 number one, and *Rare* (2020) her third consecutive chart-topping album. The Spanish-language EP *Revelación* (2021) earned a Grammy nomination for Best Latin Pop Album. "Calm Down" with Rema (2022) peaked at number three on the Hot 100. *I Said I Love You First* (2025), made with Benny Blanco, debuted at number two.
- **Television:** *Only Murders in the Building* brought her Emmy nominations as a producer and, in 2024, for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series, plus multiple Golden Globe nominations. As a producer she also backed Netflix's *13 Reasons Why* (2017–2020).
- **Film:** *Emilia Pérez* (2024) earned her a Golden Globe nomination for Best Supporting Actress.
- **Business:** Rare Beauty launched at Sephora in September 2020 and funds the Rare Impact Fund, which set a $100 million goal for youth mental-health services.

## Controversies & Criticism

Gomez has faced periodic criticism. In 2022 she said Taylor Swift was her "only friend in the industry," prompting a public rebuke from Francia Raísa, who had donated a kidney to her in 2017; the two later reconciled. Her *Emilia Pérez* performance drew criticism in Mexico over her Spanish fluency, which she acknowledged. A November 2023 Instagram post about the Israel–Gaza war, in which she said she could not "change the world," was widely criticized as evasive. In January 2025 she deleted a tearful video responding to U.S. immigration raids after backlash from both critics and supporters. Her 2016 tour cancellation and prolonged breaks from social media have also fueled tabloid speculation.

## Legacy & Influence

Gomez is a case study in sustaining relevance across formats: child actor, pop star, showrunner-level producer, and founder. Her candor about her 2017 kidney transplant, her 2020 bipolar diagnosis, and the 2022 documentary *Selena Gomez: My Mind & Me* helped normalize mental-health disclosure among celebrities. Rare Beauty's success made her one of the few entertainers to become a billionaire primarily through a consumer brand rather than music or film. She married Benny Blanco in September 2025.
