## Summary

Armin van Buuren is a Dutch DJ, producer and radio host who became the most prominent figure in trance music during the 2000s and 2010s. He was voted the world's number one DJ in the DJ Mag Top 100 poll five times (2007, 2008, 2009, 2010 and 2012), a record at the time, and his weekly radio show *A State of Trance* has run since 2001 and passed its 1,000th episode in 2021.

Beyond his own releases, van Buuren co-founded the label Armada Music in 2003, which grew into one of the largest independent dance labels in the world. His 2013 single "This Is What It Feels Like" earned a Grammy nomination and pushed him into the mainstream, while his arena-scale "Armin Only" shows set a template for solo electronic music productions.

## Early Life & Background

Armin Jozef Jacobus Daniël van Buuren was born on 25 December 1976 in Leiden, Netherlands, and grew up in nearby Koudekerk aan den Rijn. His father played the piano and introduced him to electronic pioneers such as Jean-Michel Jarre and Klaus Schulze. He also cited Dutch producer Ben Liebrand as an early influence and began experimenting with music software as a teenager. Van Buuren studied law at Leiden University, pausing his studies as his DJ career took off before completing his degree in 2003.

## Rise to Prominence

His first notable release, "Blue Fear," appeared on Cyber Records in 1995 and charted in the United Kingdom. "Communication" (1999) reached the UK Top 20 in 2000, bringing him wider recognition. In March 2001 he launched *A State of Trance* on ID&T Radio, a weekly two-hour show that became the central platform of the genre and later spawned festival events on several continents.

Van Buuren's debut album *76* arrived in 2003, followed by *Shivers* (2005). *Imagine* (2008) entered the Dutch album chart at number one, the first trance album to do so. The "Armin Only" concept, launched in 2005, saw him headline entire evenings alone, culminating in a two-night stand at the Amsterdam ArenA in May 2017.

## Key Achievements

- **DJ Mag Top 100**: Number one in 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010 and 2012, and a fixture in the top five for well over a decade.
- **Grammy nomination**: "This Is What It Feels Like" featuring Trevor Guthrie was nominated for Best Dance Recording at the 2014 ceremony.
- **Royal honours**: Appointed Officer of the Order of Orange-Nassau in April 2011. In April 2013 he performed with the Royal Concertgebouw Orchestra at the inauguration celebrations for King Willem-Alexander.
- **Albums**: *Mirage* (2010), *Intense* (2013), *Embrace* (2015), *Balance* (2019), *Feel Again* (2023) and *Breathe In* (2024).
- **Armada Music**: Co-founded in 2003 with Maykel Piron and David Lewis; the label has released work by hundreds of artists and won multiple industry awards.

## Controversies & Criticism

Van Buuren's move toward pop-leaning vocal tracks, beginning around *Intense* in 2013, drew sustained criticism from trance purists who felt he had abandoned the genre he championed. Tracks such as "Blah Blah Blah" (2018) were dismissed by some as novelty records. His long-standing reliance on studio partner Benno de Goeij prompted periodic debate about how much of his output he produces alone, a criticism common to many headline DJs. He has also spoken openly about burnout and the strain of touring, notably around the 2023 documentary accompanying *Feel Again*.

## Legacy & Influence

Van Buuren did more than any single artist to keep trance commercially viable through the 2010s, when big-room house and EDM dominated festival stages. *A State of Trance* served as an incubator for younger producers, and Armada Music became a lasting institution in dance music. His combination of radio, label, festival brand and orchestral collaborations established a business model that later DJs adopted widely.
