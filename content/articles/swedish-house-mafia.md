## Summary

Swedish House Mafia is a Swedish electronic dance music supergroup made up of Axwell (Axel Hedfors), Steve Angello (Steven Angello Josefsson Fragogiannis) and Sebastian Ingrosso. Formed in Stockholm in 2008, the trio became the most visible act of the progressive house boom that pushed dance music into stadiums and mainstream pop charts between 2010 and 2013.

Their catalogue of singles, including "One", "Save the World" and "Don't You Worry Child", helped define the festival "big room" sound. After a highly publicised split in 2013 and a reunion in 2018, the group returned with a debut studio album, *Paradise Again*, in 2022, and continues to tour arenas and headline festivals worldwide.

## Early Life & Background

All three members built solo careers in Stockholm's house scene before forming the group. Axwell, born in 1977 in Lund, ran the label Axtone and scored a UK top-20 hit with "Feel the Vibe" in 2005. Steve Angello, born in 1982 in Athens and raised in Stockholm, founded Size Records in 2003. Sebastian Ingrosso, born in 1983 in Stockholm, is Angello's childhood friend and co-founded Refune Records.

The three collaborated on remixes and one-off tracks throughout the mid-2000s, and journalists began using the "Swedish House Mafia" label for the loose Stockholm collective as early as 2005. The trio formally adopted the name in 2008.

## Rise to Prominence

Their first official single, "Leave the World Behind" with Laidback Luke and Deborah Cox, appeared in 2009. The 2010 single "One", later reworked with Pharrell Williams as "One (Your Name)", reached the UK top 10, and "Miami 2 Ibiza" with Tinie Tempah followed later that year. The compilation *Until One* was released in October 2010.

In December 2011 the group sold out Madison Square Garden in New York, a landmark booking for a dance act at the time. "Save the World" (2011) earned a Grammy nomination for Best Dance Recording, and "Don't You Worry Child" (2012), featuring John Martin, topped the UK Singles Chart and reached number six on the Billboard Hot 100.

On 24 June 2012 the trio announced they would disband after a farewell "One Last Tour", which ended with a headline set at Ultra Music Festival in Miami on 24 March 2013. The 2014 documentary *Leave the World Behind* chronicled the tour.

## Key Achievements

- **Chart success:** "Don't You Worry Child" was certified multi-platinum in several markets and remains one of the best-selling dance singles of the 2010s.
- **Reunion:** The group reunited unannounced at Ultra Miami on 25 March 2018, then signed with Republic Records in 2021.
- **Debut album:** *Paradise Again* was released on 15 April 2022, preceded by "It Gets Better", "Lifetime" and "Moth to a Flame" with The Weeknd.
- **Coachella 2022:** They co-headlined the festival's closing night with The Weeknd after Kanye West withdrew, followed by a global arena tour.

## Controversies & Criticism

The group's 2012 shows drew safety concerns. A concert at Phoenix Park in Dublin in July 2012 saw multiple stabbings and drug-related hospitalisations, prompting a public inquiry and criticism of the promoters' planning. The farewell tour was also marred by reports of friction between the members and with their then-manager, Amy Thomson, who parted ways with the group afterward.

Critics have characterised their sound as formulaic and commercially driven, pointing to the 2012 single "Greyhound", produced as an Absolut Vodka campaign, as emblematic of EDM's corporate turn. The 2018 reunion was followed by a three-year gap without new music, and their Coachella 2022 set received mixed reviews for sound problems and pacing.

## Legacy & Influence

Swedish House Mafia helped move electronic music from clubs into arenas and mainstream radio, paving the way for acts such as Avicii, Alesso and Martin Garrix. Their melodic, vocal-led production style shaped the festival main-stage sound for much of the 2010s, and their farewell and reunion stand among the most publicised events in dance music history.
